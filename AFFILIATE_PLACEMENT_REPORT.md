## 4. Claims Audit & Removals

- **Removed/Avoided claims:**
  - Avoided any claim that cooling sleepwear or bed ventilation "treats" or "stops" hot flashes. All cooling gear copy strictly states it is for surface comfort and preventing post-sweat chills.
  - Avoided any claim that pelvic floor exercises or biofeedback devices "cure" urge incontinence or repair anatomical pelvic organ prolapse.
  - Avoided claims that hyaluronic acid or polycarbophil vaginal moisturisers "reverse" cellular atrophy or replace prescription estrogen.
  - Avoided claiming that any book represents absolute clinical authority; clearly noted limitations and stated that readers should consult their personal physician for individualized treatment plans.
  - Screened out banned words ("cure", "miracle", "clinically proven", "balance your hormones", "doctors don't want you to know").
