MenoVerdict is free. There are no ads, no affiliate links and no sponsors on this site, and nobody pays to be mentioned or graded.

## What that means for you

- Every article and every tool is free to use, with no login.
- We don't sell or recommend products to buy, and no link on this site earns us money.
- Evidence grades come from published research and clinical guidelines alone. You can read [how we grade the evidence](/evidence/how-we-grade/) and our [editorial policy](/evidence/editorial-policy/).
- Your tracker data stays on your device. See our [privacy policy](/privacy/).

If that ever changes, we'll say so on this page first.
