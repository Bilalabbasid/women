**We are not doctors.** MenoVerdict is an independent editorial team. We aren't a medical service, and no doctor writes our pages.

## How this site works

- **We gather what's already published.** Medical society guidelines, research studies, government health pages and medicine labels, collected in one place and written in plain language. Every page links its sources under References.
- **AI helps us summarize and grade.** AI tools help us read the research, draft summaries and apply our [evidence grading method](/evidence/how-we-grade/). AI and people both make mistakes, so anything here can be wrong, incomplete or out of date.
- **We say so on every page.** Each guide carries a "How this page was made" notice and the date it was last checked.
- **We tell you to see a doctor.** Only a doctor who knows your health history can tell you what's going on. Use this site to prepare for that appointment, not to replace it.

## What you can rely on

1. **No login and no tracking.** Every article and tool works without an account, and the site runs no analytics, ads or tracking pixels. See our [privacy policy](/privacy/).
2. **Your data stays on your device.** What you log in the tracker and tools is saved in your own browser and never sent to us.
3. **Evidence before commission.** A grade is set from the evidence before we look at whether any product or service pays a commission, and a commission never changes it. See [how we make money](/about/how-we-make-money/).
4. **No faked medical review.** A page only says it was checked by a doctor when a named, verified clinician has checked that exact version. See our [review process](/evidence/medical-review/).

## We're looking for a clinical reviewer

We'd like a Menopause Society Certified Practitioner to check our pages. Until then, every page says plainly that no doctor has checked it. When a clinician does check a page, their name, credentials and the date will appear on it, and only on that exact version.

## Tell us when we're wrong

If you spot a mistake, a broken source or something out of date, please [contact us](/contact/). Corrections are listed in our [corrections log](/evidence/updates/).
