What HRT costs depends mostly on the form, the dose and your insurance. As of November 2026, GoodRx listed generic estradiol patches at about $37 to $69 for four weekly patches, and 90 estradiol tablets at about $20 to $47, without insurance [@goodrx-estradiol-pricing-2026; @goodrx-estradiol-prices-2026-11-08]. Check the price of your exact prescription before you fill it.

## How much does HRT cost without insurance?

Without insurance, a pack of generic estradiol pills, patches or gel cost about $20 to $189 in November 2026, going by GoodRx's average retail prices, depending on the form and dose [@goodrx-estradiol-prices-2026-11-08]. Pills were the cheapest, and some gel packs cost far more. If your prescription also includes progesterone, add its price too.

These are GoodRx's average US retail prices, which is what people pay without insurance or a coupon [@goodrx-estradiol-prices-2026-11-08; @goodrx-progesterone-pricing-2026]:

| Medicine (generic) | Pack priced | Average retail price |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Estradiol tablets | 90 tablets (0.5 mg to 2 mg) | $19.94 to $47.05 [@goodrx-estradiol-prices-2026-11-08] |
| Estradiol patches, once weekly | 4 patches (about 4 weeks) | $37.13 to $68.95 [@goodrx-estradiol-prices-2026-11-08] |
| Estradiol gel packets | 30 packets | $37.69 to $189.49 [@goodrx-estradiol-prices-2026-11-08] |
| Estradiol vaginal inserts | 8 inserts (10 mcg) | $75.18 [@goodrx-estradiol-prices-2026-11-08] |
| Estradiol vaginal cream | 1 tube (42.5 g) | $310.35 [@goodrx-estradiol-prices-2026-11-08] |
| Progesterone capsules | 30 capsules (100 mg) | $22.31 [@goodrx-progesterone-pricing-2026] |
| Progesterone capsules | 90 capsules (200 mg) | $139.95 [@goodrx-progesterone-pricing-2026] |

How long a pack lasts depends on your dose, so compare the price per month for your own prescription. For what each form is and how they differ, see our guide to [types of hormone therapy](/treatments/hormone-therapy/types/). For why progesterone is part of many prescriptions, see [progesterone in HRT](/treatments/hormone-therapy/progesterone/).

## Why do HRT prices vary so much?

The same hormone can cost very different amounts because of the details of the prescription. GoodRx's averages also don't reflect the price at any one pharmacy near you [@goodrx-estradiol-prices-2026-11-08]. The main things that change the price:

- **Form**: pills, patches, gel and vaginal products are priced very differently from each other [@goodrx-estradiol-prices-2026-11-08]
- **Dose and pack size**: one estradiol gel pack of 90 packets averaged $569.81, while 30 packets of most strengths were under $40 [@goodrx-estradiol-prices-2026-11-08]
- **Brand or generic**: estradiol and progesterone both come in generic and brand versions, and GoodRx suggests asking your prescriber about less expensive generic options [@goodrx-estradiol-prices-2026-11-08; @goodrx-progesterone-pricing-2026]
- **Pharmacy and coupons**: prices differ between pharmacies, so the same prescription can cost more in one store than another [@goodrx-estradiol-prices-2026-11-08]
- **Date**: prices change, and this page shows what we checked on the date noted

## How much does a menopause telehealth visit cost?

Self-pay prices for menopause-focused telehealth ranged from a $35/month membership to $250 for a first visit when we checked in November 2026, depending on the service and whether you pay per visit or by membership. These were the self-pay prices each company listed:

- **[Midi Health](/compare/midi-review/)**: $250 for a first visit and $150 for follow-ups, not including labs or medications, with no membership required. Midi says insured patients pay about $50 per visit on average when in-network [@midi-health-pricing-2026; @midi-health-own-site-2026]
- **[Evernow](/compare/evernow-review/)**: a membership starting at **$35/month** (includes ongoing messaging and optional insurance-eligible video visits), or a one-time insurance-eligible visit with no membership [@evernow-pricing-2026; @evernow-own-site-2026]
- **[Alloy](/compare/alloy-review/)**: does not bill insurance; HSA or FSA funds can be used, and some patients get reimbursed by PPO insurance afterward. We could not confirm a specific current consult price on Alloy's public pages [@alloy-insurance-2026; @alloy-own-site-2026]

Midi's visit prices don't include medicine, so ask any service whether medicine is included. See our [menopause telehealth comparisons](/compare/) for how these services differ beyond price.

## How do I compare a clinic's price, like Elite HRT?

We don't review Elite HRT, and we couldn't find a published price for menopause hormone therapy from it. When we checked its own website in November 2026, the homepage described peptide therapy and listed no prices for menopause HRT or visits [@elite-hrt-own-site-2026]. For Elite HRT or any clinic, ask for a written, itemized quote that covers:

- **Visits**: the first visit and each follow-up
- **Lab tests**: whether they are required, and what they cost
- **Medicine**: the exact drug, dose and form, and whether it is an FDA-approved product or a compounded one (see [bioidentical vs. compounded hormones](/treatments/hormone-therapy/bioidentical-vs-compounded/))
- **Membership or contract**: any monthly fee or minimum length
- **Insurance**: whether they bill your plan, give paperwork for you to claim yourself, or accept HSA or FSA funds

Then compare that total with the pharmacy price of the same prescription from your own doctor.

## How can I pay less for HRT?

Start by comparing the cash price with your insurance price for the exact prescription you were given. Most savings come from the form, the pack size and where you fill it. Ways to check:

- **Ask the pharmacy for both prices**: the cash price and your insurance copay can differ, so ask for each
- **Ask about a 90-day supply**: GoodRx notes it may lower your total cost, but it needs a prescription written for 90 days [@goodrx-estradiol-prices-2026-11-08]
- **Ask about other forms**: ask your prescriber whether a different form (pill, patch or gel) would suit you, since prices differ between forms [@goodrx-estradiol-prices-2026-11-08]
- **Check your plan's coverage**: all US Marketplace plans cover prescription drugs [@healthcare-gov-essential-health-benefits]; our [insurance and menopause care guide](/care/insurance/) explains drug lists, generic vs. brand pricing and how to ask for an exception
