Getting effective menopause care starts with knowing your options, understanding what the evidence supports, and having practical tools to communicate with healthcare providers [@nams-2022-hormone-therapy]. 

Whether you are preparing for a first discussion about hormone therapy, looking for non-hormonal comfort strategies, or choosing evidence-based books to deepen your understanding, this section brings together practical guides to help you make informed care decisions with confidence [@nice-ng23].

## Care guides and practical support

- **[Vaginal moisturisers and lubricants](/care/moisturizers-and-lubricants/)**: How continuous tissue hydrators and intercourse lubricants differ, how to choose pH-balanced formulas, and when prescription treatment is needed.
- **[Evidence-based menopause books](/care/menopause-books/)**: Thorough reviews of doctor-authored guides that break down the science of perimenopause, hormones, and fitness without wellness marketing.
- **[How to do pelvic floor exercises correctly](/care/pelvic-floor-exercises/)**: A step-by-step method for muscle training at home, common mistakes to avoid, and guidance on biofeedback devices.

## Preparing for clinical appointments

Many women find that short medical appointments leave little time to discuss complex, fluctuating symptoms. Tracking your symptoms over several weeks gives you and your clinician concrete patterns rather than vague recollections.

Use our [symptom tracker and doctor summary](/tools/symptom-tracker/) on your device to log daily patterns and generate a concise one-page summary for your next visit. If you want structured questions to ask, explore our guide on [questions to ask your doctor about perimenopause](/perimenopause/questions-for-your-doctor/).
