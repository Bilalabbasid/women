For night sweats, wear loose, lightweight sleepwear in a breathable fabric, in layers you can take off quickly [@nams-2023-nonhormone-therapy; @nia-hot-flashes-what-can-i-do]. No fabric has been proven best: the few fabric studies were small, and none included women with night sweats [@li-sleepwear-bedding-fibre-review-2024]. Treatment, not pajamas, reduces night sweats [@nams-2022-hormone-therapy; @nams-2023-nonhormone-therapy].

Night sweats are hot flashes that happen while you sleep, and heavy sweating and cold shivering can follow [@nia-what-is-menopause]. The right sleepwear can make that more bearable. It won't make night sweats happen less often [@nams-2023-nonhormone-therapy].

## What is the best sleepwear for night sweats?

The best sleepwear for night sweats is light, loose and breathable, and easy to take off when a hot flash starts. The Menopause Society, the National Institute on Aging and the NHS all suggest simple clothing changes rather than any special product [@nams-2023-nonhormone-therapy; @nia-hot-flashes-what-can-i-do; @nhs-menopause-things-you-can-do].

- **Lightweight**: wear light clothing to bed [@nhs-menopause-things-you-can-do].
- **Layers you can take off**: dress in layers that you can remove at the start of a hot flash [@nia-hot-flashes-what-can-i-do].
- **Sleeveless or short-sleeved tops**: sleeveless tops are one of the clothing changes experts list [@nams-2023-nonhormone-therapy].
- **Breathable fabric**: choose breathable clothing materials [@nams-2023-nonhormone-therapy].
- **Nothing heavy over your head**: avoid pullover sweaters and tops [@nams-2023-nonhormone-therapy].

## Which fabric is best for night sweats?

Nobody knows for sure, because no study has compared fabrics in women with menopause night sweats [@li-sleepwear-bedding-fibre-review-2024]. A 2024 review found only nine small studies of sleepwear and bedding fabrics, in babies, children, young adults and older adults [@li-sleepwear-bedding-fibre-review-2024]. Here is what they found, with cotton usually as the comparison:

| Fabric | What the small sleep studies found | Who was studied |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Wool | People fell asleep faster than in cotton [@li-sleepwear-bedding-fibre-review-2024] | Young adults in a cool room; older adults in a warm room [@li-sleepwear-bedding-fibre-review-2024] |
| Polyester | No overall difference from cotton [@li-sleepwear-bedding-fibre-review-2024] | Older adults [@li-sleepwear-bedding-fibre-review-2024] |
| Quick-drying synthetic blend | More deep sleep than cotton [@li-sleepwear-bedding-fibre-review-2024] | Girls around age 12, one study [@li-sleepwear-bedding-fibre-review-2024] |
| Bamboo blend | No consistent results [@li-sleepwear-bedding-fibre-review-2024] | Babies only [@li-sleepwear-bedding-fibre-review-2024] |
| Silk | Not tested [@li-sleepwear-bedding-fibre-review-2024] | No studies [@li-sleepwear-bedding-fibre-review-2024] |

Read the wool results with care. The review's authors received research funding from a wool industry body, the studies were small and varied, and they concluded it is hard to draw an overall conclusion [@li-sleepwear-bedding-fibre-review-2024].

### Is cotton bad for night sweats?

Not necessarily: in the one study that compared them in older adults, cotton did no worse overall than polyester [@li-sleepwear-bedding-fibre-review-2024]. Cotton and wool do conduct heat much more once they've soaked up moisture [@li-sleepwear-bedding-fibre-review-2024], and chills often follow a night sweat [@nia-what-is-menopause]. Light layers you can take off or swap may suit you better than one heavy top [@nia-hot-flashes-what-can-i-do].

## Do cooling pajamas work for menopause?

Cooling pajamas may feel more comfortable, but there's no good evidence that cooling products reduce hot flashes or night sweats [@nams-2023-nonhormone-therapy]. In a small trial of a nighttime cooling device, women said they slept better, but their night-time hot flashes were no fewer and no shorter [@nams-2023-nonhormone-therapy]. Experts say cooling ideas still need larger, properly controlled trials [@nams-2023-nonhormone-therapy].

Cooling sleepwear, bedding and fans: :grade{id="cooling-comfort"}. "Cooling" or "moisture-wicking" on a label hasn't been tested against menopause night sweats, so check the fabric content and choose what feels light on your skin [@li-sleepwear-bedding-fibre-review-2024].

## What bedding helps with night sweats?

Layered bedding you can push off or pull back, plus a fan and a cooler bedroom, are the simplest changes [@nia-hot-flashes-what-can-i-do; @nhs-menopause-things-you-can-do]. Other ideas experts list include a cold pack under your pillow, turning your pillow when it feels warm, and a dual-control electric blanket or a bed fan if you share a bed [@nams-2023-nonhormone-therapy].

Studies of sheets and duvets are even thinner than for sleepwear. One study found linen sheets improved sleep in warm conditions in young adults, though the bed pad used may have mattered too, and wool and polyester quilts made no difference [@li-sleepwear-bedding-fibre-review-2024].

:::try
1. Lower the temperature in your bedroom and turn on a fan [@nia-hot-flashes-what-can-i-do].
2. Layer your bedding so you can push a layer off or pull it back [@nia-hot-flashes-what-can-i-do].
3. Wear light layers you can take off when a hot flash starts [@nia-hot-flashes-what-can-i-do; @nhs-menopause-things-you-can-do].
4. Keep a cold drink by the bed, and try small amounts of cold water before sleep [@nia-hot-flashes-what-can-i-do; @nhs-menopause-things-you-can-do].
5. Put a cold pack under your pillow, or turn the pillow over when it gets warm [@nams-2023-nonhormone-therapy].
:::

## What helps night sweats more than sleepwear?

Treatment helps more. Hormone therapy is the most effective: compared with placebo, it cut how often hot flashes and night sweats happened by about 75% [@nams-2022-hormone-therapy].

- **Hormone therapy (HRT)**: :grade{id="mht-vms"}. The most effective treatment for hot flashes and night sweats [@nams-2022-hormone-therapy].
- **Non-hormone prescriptions**: :grade{id="nonhormonal-rx-vms"}. The Menopause Society recommends fezolinetant, SSRIs and SNRIs, and gabapentin [@nams-2023-nonhormone-therapy]. See [non-hormonal treatments for hot flashes](/treatments/nonhormonal/).
- **CBT and clinical hypnosis**: :grade{id="cbt-hypnosis-vms"}. CBT reduces how much hot flashes bother you, and hypnosis reduces how often they happen [@nams-2023-nonhormone-therapy].

## When to see a doctor about night sweats

See a doctor if night sweats happen regularly, wake you up or worry you [@nhs-night-sweats; @mayo-night-sweats-when-to-see-doctor]. Make an appointment soon if they come with any of these:

- a fever, or feeling hot, cold or shivery [@nhs-night-sweats; @mayo-night-sweats-when-to-see-doctor]
- weight loss you can't explain [@nhs-night-sweats; @mayo-night-sweats-when-to-see-doctor]
- a cough, diarrhea or pain in one spot [@mayo-night-sweats-when-to-see-doctor]
- night sweats that start months or years after your menopause symptoms ended [@mayo-night-sweats-when-to-see-doctor]
