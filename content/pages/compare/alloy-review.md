**What it is:** Alloy is a direct-to-consumer telehealth company offering hormone therapy and a range of other midlife wellness products, with care from board-certified physicians who the company describes as menopause-specialized. We reviewed Alloy's own site directly; this isn't a paid placement, and nobody pays to be reviewed here.

## What Alloy actually offers

Based on Alloy's own site, you complete a medical intake (the company states 5–10 minutes), then a physician reviews it and creates a treatment plan, typically within about 12 hours. Hormone therapy is available as pills, patches, gels, sprays, and creams, with free delivery and 24/7 messaging access to the care team [@alloy-own-site-2026]. Beyond hormone therapy, Alloy also sells skincare, sexual health products, GLP-1 weight-care treatment, hair/lash/brow treatments, and a gut-health supplement — worth knowing if you're specifically looking for menopause hormone care rather than a broader product catalog [@alloy-own-site-2026].

## Cost and insurance, as of November 2026

Alloy explicitly markets itself as a **cash-pay model**, positioning this as a way to avoid insurance pre-authorization delays and "hidden fees" — it does not bill insurance directly [@alloy-own-site-2026; @alloy-insurance-2026]. FSA and HSA cards are accepted for payment. We were not able to independently confirm a specific, current price for a medical consultation on Alloy's public pages at the time of this review; check Alloy's site directly at checkout for the exact current cost of a consultation and any ongoing product subscription, since these can change and may vary by promotion.

## Who this may suit

If you prefer a cash-pay model with no insurance paperwork, and you're comfortable with an asynchronous intake rather than a scheduled live video visit, Alloy's model is built around speed and convenience.

## Who this may not suit

If you want your visit billed to insurance, Alloy's model won't do that for you — compare this against [Midi Health](/compare/midi-review/), which does work with many insurance plans. If your main interest is non-hormonal or complex symptom management, note that Alloy's broader product catalog spans well beyond menopause-specific care, which is worth being clear-eyed about before signing up.
