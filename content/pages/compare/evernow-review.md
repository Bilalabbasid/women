**What it is:** Evernow is a telehealth company offering menopause and perimenopause care, with two distinct pricing paths: a one-time visit, or an ongoing membership. We reviewed Evernow's own site directly; this isn't a paid placement, and nothing here is ranked by commission.

## What Evernow actually offers

Based on Evernow's own site, care is delivered by a team the company describes as specially certified to treat women in menopause and postmenopause, covering hormone therapy alongside symptom categories like sexual health, body aches, mental health, weight, hair and skin changes, and sleep issues [@evernow-own-site-2026]. Evernow also sells supplements marketed to complement hormone therapy (for sleep, mood, energy, skin, and metabolism) [@evernow-own-site-2026].

## Cost and insurance, as of November 2026

Evernow offers two paths [@evernow-own-site-2026]:

- **Pay per visit:** A one-time virtual visit with a menopause provider, described as insurance-eligible, with no membership required.
- **Membership:** Ongoing messaging access to a clinician, optional insurance-eligible virtual visits, ongoing prescriptions, and care plans, starting at **$35/month**.

Evernow states it is now accepting insurance for video visits. As with any insurance-eligible service, your actual out-of-pocket cost depends on your specific plan's deductible, copay, and coinsurance — confirm directly with Evernow before booking.

## Who this may suit

If you want the flexibility to try a single visit before committing to ongoing care, or you want the option to message a clinician between visits without paying per message, Evernow's two-tier model offers that flexibility explicitly.

## Who this may not suit

If you specifically need your care billed to insurance for every interaction, confirm exactly which parts of the membership (messaging vs. video visits) are actually insurance-eligible versus bundled into the $35/month fee, since the site describes video visits as only "optional" and "insurance-eligible" within the membership — not automatically covered.

## What we didn't evaluate

We did not independently verify clinical outcomes or the specific supplement formulations sold alongside hormone therapy. We also did not repeat customer testimonials from Evernow's site, since we have no way to independently verify them. This review describes Evernow's own stated service and pricing model, not a judgment of care quality.
