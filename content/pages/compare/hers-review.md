## What's generally understood about Hers' menopause care

Hers operates under **Hims & Hers Health, Inc.** (NYSE: HIMS), a publicly traded telehealth platform founded in 2017 that connects patients with providers across several specialties — including erectile dysfunction, hair loss, skincare, mental health, and weight loss — alongside Hers' own women's health offerings [@hims-hers-health-public-company-2026]. Menopause care is one category within that broader platform, rather than a menopause-exclusive clinic's sole focus. Beyond that general positioning, we are not stating specific pricing, insurance policies, or prescribing details here, because we could not verify them directly ourselves at the time of this review.

## What to check directly before choosing Hers

- **Current pricing** for a menopause-specific visit and any ongoing membership or medication costs
- **Whether Hers bills insurance directly**, or operates on a cash-pay basis
- **Which specific medications and formats** (hormonal and non-hormonal) are available for menopause symptoms
- **Whether your assigned clinician specializes in menopause**, given that Hers covers multiple health categories

## Who this may suit

If you're already a Hers or Hims customer for another service and want to explore menopause care within a platform you're familiar with, it may be worth a direct look at their current offering.
