Midi Health and Alloy are both online menopause services, but you pay for them differently. Midi bills most major PPO insurance plans for virtual visits, or charges $250 for a first visit if you pay yourself. Alloy doesn't bill insurance and prices each medicine, with estradiol from $39.99 a month [@midi-health-pricing-2026; @alloy-insurance-2026; @alloy-solutions-pricing-2026].

All details below come from each company's own site, checked in November 2026. Nobody pays to be listed here, and this isn't a recommendation of either.

## Does Midi Health or Alloy take insurance?

Midi takes insurance and Alloy doesn't. Midi says it's in-network with most, though not all, major PPO plans, and that insured patients pay about $50 out of pocket per visit on average [@midi-health-own-site-2026]. Alloy says it doesn't bill insurance for any service or prescription, though some patients get reimbursed by a PPO plan after paying [@alloy-insurance-2026].

Midi doesn't accept Medicaid or Medi-Cal, and sees Medicare patients as self-pay only [@midi-health-own-site-2026]. For more on what plans usually cover, see [does insurance cover menopause care?](/care/insurance/)

## Which is cheaper, Midi or Alloy?

It depends on your insurance and the medicine you're prescribed. Midi lists $250 for a first visit and $150 for a follow-up if you pay yourself, not counting labs or medicines; with an in-network plan, Midi says the average is about $50 per visit [@midi-health-pricing-2026; @midi-health-own-site-2026].

Alloy lists each medicine's price and usually bills every 3 months [@alloy-solutions-pricing-2026; @alloy-cost-help-2026]:

- **Estradiol pill**: from $39.99 a month [@alloy-solutions-pricing-2026]
- **Estradiol gel or spray**: $69.99 a month [@alloy-solutions-pricing-2026]
- **Estradiol patch**: $100 a month [@alloy-solutions-pricing-2026]
- **Progesterone**: from $23 a month [@alloy-solutions-pricing-2026]

Alloy also charges for a consultation before hormone treatment, but the pages we checked don't state the amount [@alloy-after-signup-2026]. To compare, add up a year of visits and medicine for each. Our [HRT cost guide](/care/hrt-cost/) shows what the same medicines can cost at a regular pharmacy.

## What's a Midi visit like, and what's an Alloy visit like?

A Midi visit is a live virtual appointment; Alloy has no live visit by default. Midi's first visit lasts 30 minutes and covers your symptoms, history and goals, and a 15-minute returning visit checks progress [@midi-health-pricing-2026]. With Alloy, you fill in a 5 to 10 minute medical form, a doctor reviews it, and you can message the care team afterward [@alloy-own-site-2026].

Alloy says hormone prescriptions don't ship until you approve the payment [@alloy-after-signup-2026]. If you want to talk through a complex history in real time, a live visit allows that; if you'd rather not schedule a call, a form may suit you.

## What do Midi and Alloy each treat?

Both treat menopause symptoms with hormone therapy, but their range differs. Midi says its clinicians build a care plan that can include hormone therapy, non-hormone prescriptions, lab tests and lifestyle advice [@midi-health-own-site-2026]. Alloy lists estradiol pills, patches, gels and sprays, progesterone, vaginal estradiol cream and non-hormonal paroxetine, plus skincare, sexual health, weight, hair and gut products [@alloy-solutions-pricing-2026; @alloy-own-site-2026].

Alloy says its estradiol is FDA-approved [@alloy-bioidentical-2026]. Whatever the service, hormone therapy is the most effective treatment for hot flashes and night sweats :grade{id="mht-vms"}, with benefits that outweigh risks for most healthy women under 60 or within 10 years of menopause [@nams-2022-hormone-therapy]. See [hormone therapy](/treatments/hormone-therapy/).

## Can I use my local pharmacy?

With Alloy, not at first. Alloy says it can only send a new prescription to its partner pharmacy; after at least one order, refills can be moved elsewhere, but you must cancel your Alloy subscription and lose its doctor messaging [@alloy-pharmacy-transfer-2026]. Midi's pricing page doesn't say how prescriptions are filled, so ask before you book [@midi-health-pricing-2026].

## What is Midi Health?

Midi Health is a US telehealth clinic for perimenopause and menopause. It says it offers virtual visits with doctors and nurse practitioners in all 50 states, and builds a care plan that may include hormone and non-hormone treatment [@midi-health-own-site-2026]. See our full [Midi Health review](/compare/midi-review/), our [Alloy review](/compare/alloy-review/), or [Midi vs. Evernow](/compare/midi-vs-evernow/).

## Things that may matter to you

- **If you have a PPO plan**: check whether Midi is in-network for you, since Alloy won't bill insurance [@midi-health-pricing-2026; @alloy-insurance-2026].
- **If you pay cash either way**: compare Midi's visit fees against Alloy's consultation plus medicine prices at checkout [@midi-health-pricing-2026; @alloy-solutions-pricing-2026].
- **If you want a live conversation**: Midi's visits are live; Alloy's intake is a form [@midi-health-pricing-2026; @alloy-own-site-2026].
