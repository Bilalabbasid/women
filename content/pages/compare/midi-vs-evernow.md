Midi Health and Evernow both offer online menopause care and both bill insurance for visits, but they charge in different ways. Midi works per visit: in-network with most PPO plans, or $250 for a first visit if you pay yourself. Evernow offers single visits ($150 self-pay) or a membership with messaging from $35 a month [@midi-health-pricing-2026; @evernow-faq-2026].

All details below come from each company's own pages, checked in November 2026. Nobody pays to be listed here, and this isn't a recommendation of either.

## Midi vs. Evernow at a glance

| | Midi Health | Evernow |

| **Medicare and Medicaid** | Medicare self-pay only; no Medicaid or Medi-Cal [@midi-health-own-site-2026] | Not currently supported [@evernow-faq-2026] |

## Does Midi or Evernow take insurance?

Both do, for visits. Midi says it's in-network with most, though not all, major PPO plans, and that insured patients pay about $50 out of pocket per visit on average; your deductible can mean paying up to $250 for a new visit or $150 for a follow-up [@midi-health-own-site-2026]. Evernow says it covers video visits through major national commercial plans, naming UnitedHealthcare, Aetna, Anthem and Blue Cross Blue Shield [@evernow-faq-2026].

The difference is in what insurance doesn't cover. Evernow's membership fee isn't insurance-covered, though HSA or FSA funds can pay it [@evernow-faq-2026]. Midi has no membership; it accepts HSA and FSA payment too [@midi-health-pricing-2026]. Neither bills Medicare or Medicaid [@midi-health-own-site-2026; @evernow-faq-2026]. See [does insurance cover menopause care?](/care/insurance/)

## How much do Midi and Evernow cost without insurance?

A self-pay first visit costs $250 at Midi and $150 at Evernow, according to each company [@midi-health-pricing-2026; @evernow-faq-2026]. Midi's follow-ups are $150, and its fees don't include labs or medicines [@midi-health-own-site-2026]. Evernow's membership options are [@evernow-faq-2026; @evernow-pricing-2026]:

- **Month to month**: $49 a month
- **3 months**: $129, about $43 a month
- **12 months**: $420, about $35 a month

Evernow's single-visit option includes a 20-minute consult plus 90 days of access to your care plan and prescriptions [@evernow-membership-vs-ppv-2025]. Medicines are billed on top at both services [@midi-health-own-site-2026; @evernow-faq-2026]. Our [HRT cost guide](/care/hrt-cost/) shows typical medicine prices.

## What are the visits like?

Both use live video visits, but the length and follow-up differ. Midi's first visit lasts 30 minutes and covers your symptoms, history and goals, and a 15-minute returning visit checks how things are going [@midi-health-pricing-2026]. Evernow's single visit is a 20-minute consult [@evernow-membership-vs-ppv-2025], and members can message their care team at any hour, with replies often within a day [@evernow-faq-2026].

Evernow says it can often book a video visit within 24 hours [@evernow-faq-2026]. Midi's pricing page doesn't publish a wait time, so check open appointments when you book [@midi-health-pricing-2026].

## What do Midi and Evernow treat?

Both focus on perimenopause and menopause and offer hormone therapy. Midi says its clinicians, including doctors and nurse practitioners, build a care plan that can include hormone therapy, non-hormone prescriptions, lab tests and lifestyle advice [@midi-health-own-site-2026]. Evernow says its clinicians can prescribe hormone therapy, GLP-1 weight-loss medicines, hair loss treatment, prescription skincare, vaginal products and anxiety medicines [@evernow-faq-2026]. Evernow also sells supplements it markets alongside hormone therapy [@evernow-own-site-2026].

Hormone therapy is the most effective treatment for hot flashes and night sweats :grade{id="mht-vms"}, and for most healthy women under 60 or within 10 years of menopause its benefits outweigh its risks [@nams-2022-hormone-therapy]. See [hormone therapy](/treatments/hormone-therapy/).

## Where do prescriptions go?

Evernow says prescriptions can go to your local pharmacy or to its partner pharmacies, though some cash-pay-only medicines come from its delivery pharmacy [@evernow-faq-2026]. Midi's pricing page doesn't say how prescriptions are filled, so ask before your visit [@midi-health-pricing-2026].

## Things that may matter to you

- **If you have a PPO plan**: check whether Midi is in-network for you and whether Evernow covers your plan [@midi-health-pricing-2026; @evernow-faq-2026].
- **If you have Medicare**: Midi will see you as self-pay; Evernow doesn't support Medicare plans [@midi-health-own-site-2026; @evernow-faq-2026].
- **If you want help between visits**: Evernow's membership includes messaging [@evernow-faq-2026].
- **If you'd rather not sign up for anything recurring**: Midi charges per visit, and Evernow also sells single visits [@midi-health-pricing-2026; @evernow-faq-2026].
