**Quick answer:** If you want a straightforward insurance-billed video visit with no subscription, Midi fits that model directly. If you want ongoing messaging access to a clinician between visits, Evernow's membership offers that flexibility, in addition to its own one-time visit option.

## Pricing models compared

- **Midi Health:** In-network with most major PPO plans; self-pay pricing of **$250 (first visit) / $150 (follow-up)**, not including labs or medications. Insured patients reportedly average around $50 out-of-pocket per visit [@midi-health-own-site-2026]. No membership fee. See our full [Midi Health review](/compare/midi-review/).
- **Evernow:** Offers **two paths**: a one-time, insurance-eligible visit with no membership required, or a **membership starting at $35/month** that includes ongoing messaging access to a clinician and optional insurance-eligible video visits [@evernow-own-site-2026]. See our full [Evernow review](/compare/evernow-review/).

The key practical difference: Evernow's membership bundles ongoing messaging access for a flat monthly fee, while Midi's model is purely per-visit.

## What each covers

Midi's Care Plan can include hormone therapy, non-hormonal prescriptions, labs, and lifestyle guidance. Evernow covers hormone therapy alongside a broader set of symptom categories (sexual health, body aches, mental health, weight, hair/skin, sleep) and also sells supplements marketed to complement hormone therapy [@evernow-own-site-2026; @midi-health-own-site-2026].

## Who each suits

- **Choose Midi if:** You want a single, insurance-billed video visit without an ongoing subscription commitment.
- **Choose Evernow if:** You want the option of ongoing messaging access to a clinician between visits, or you'd rather try a single visit before committing to anything recurring.
