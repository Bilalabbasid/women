We welcome questions about our sources, requests for corrections, accessibility feedback, and privacy inquiries.

## Urgent medical or crisis situations

**MenoVerdict cannot provide medical advice or emergency support.** We are an editorial team, not a medical clinic, and we cannot answer questions about your personal symptoms, lab results, or prescriptions.

- If you have severe or sudden symptoms (such as chest pain, shortness of breath, fainting, or heavy bleeding), seek emergency medical care immediately.
- If you are in distress or thinking about harming yourself, visit our [crisis and urgent help page](/urgent-help/) for free, confidential 24/7 support lines in the US (`988`) and the UK (`116 123`).

## How to reach the editorial team

- **Editorial corrections and source inquiries:** `editorial@menoverdict.com` (Please include the page URL and the specific claim or citation you are writing about. We review every factual query against our [editorial policy](/evidence/editorial-policy/) and log confirmed fixes on our [corrections and updates page](/evidence/updates/).)
- **Privacy and data rights:** `privacy@menoverdict.com` (Note: by default, your entries in our [symptom tracker](/tools/symptom-tracker/) and [symptoms checklist](/tools/symptoms-checklist/) live only in your own browser on your device, so we cannot see or access them. You can export or delete them at any time directly inside the tool. Read our [privacy policy](/privacy/) for details.)
- **General inquiries and partnerships:** `contact@menoverdict.com` (See [how we make money](/about/how-we-make-money/) for our strict separation between commercial partnerships and [evidence grades](/evidence/how-we-grade/).)
