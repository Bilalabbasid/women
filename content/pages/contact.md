We welcome questions about our sources, requests for corrections, accessibility feedback, and privacy inquiries.

## Urgent medical or crisis situations

**MenoVerdict cannot provide medical advice or emergency support.** We are an editorial team, not a medical clinic, and we cannot answer questions about your personal symptoms, lab results, or prescriptions.

- If you have severe or sudden symptoms (such as chest pain, shortness of breath, fainting, or heavy bleeding), call 911 or seek emergency medical care immediately.
- If you are in distress or thinking about harming yourself, call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline), free, confidential and 24/7. More support lines are on our [crisis and urgent help page](/urgent-help/).
- If you're in the UK: call 999 for an emergency or NHS 111 for urgent medical help, and Samaritans are on 116 123, free and 24/7.

## How to reach us

Every inquiry below goes to the same mailbox: **`contact@menoverdict.com`**.

- **Editorial corrections and source inquiries:** Please include the page URL and the specific claim or citation you're writing about. We review every factual query against our [editorial policy](/evidence/editorial-policy/) and log confirmed fixes on our [corrections and updates page](/evidence/updates/).
- **Privacy and data rights:** By default, your entries in our [symptom tracker](/tools/symptom-tracker/) and [symptoms checklist](/tools/symptoms-checklist/) live only in your own browser on your device, so we cannot see or access them. You can export or delete them at any time directly inside the tool. Read our [privacy policy](/privacy/) for details.
