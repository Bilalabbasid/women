**Last updated: November 7, 2026.** This page lists everything the site stores in your browser, taken from the site's code.

## We don't set cookies

MenoVerdict sets no cookies of any kind: no analytics, advertising, social media or session cookies. There are no accounts, so there's nothing to keep you signed in.

## Your choice

The first time you visit, we ask whether we may remember a few preferences on this device. Both answers work the same way for everything you log or save. You can change your answer at any time with **Storage choices** at the bottom of every page.

- **Essential only:** we keep only what you explicitly log or save, plus your answer. Any preferences already stored are deleted.
- **Allow preferences:** we also remember small conveniences, listed below.

## Essential: what you asked the site to keep

These are stored in your browser's own storage (IndexedDB and local storage, which are not cookies) because you entered or saved them, and the tools can't work without them. None of it is sent to us.

| What | Where | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Your tracker entries, treatments, saved tool answers, doctor-report questions and reminder settings | IndexedDB (`menowell_local_db`) | You entered them and asked the app to keep them |
| Symptoms you pinned | Local storage (`menowell_pinned_symptoms`) | You pinned them in the checklist or onboarding |
| Pages you saved for later | Local storage (`menowell_bookmarks`) | You pressed "Save for later" |
| Your storage choice | Local storage (`menoverdict_storage_consent`) | So we don't ask you again on every page |

## Preferences: only if you allow them

| What | Where | Why |
|---|---|---|
| That you have used a tool | Local storage (`menowell_tool_used`) | So the offer to install the app only appears after you've used it, never on a first visit |
| Messages you closed, such as the catch-up reminder or the install offer | Local storage and IndexedDB | So a message you closed doesn't come back |

Nothing is stored to measure you, count your visits or recognize you. You can delete everything at any time from the app's settings, or by clearing this site's data in your browser.

## Other websites

When you click a link to another website, such as a telehealth service or a research publisher, that site may set its own cookies. Its own cookie and privacy policies apply there.

See also our [privacy policy](/privacy/).
