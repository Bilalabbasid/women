**Last updated: November 7, 2026.** This page lists everything the site stores in your browser, taken from the site's code.

## We don't set cookies

MenoVerdict sets no cookies of any kind: no analytics, advertising, social media or session cookies. There are no accounts, so there's nothing to keep you signed in.

## What we store on your device, and why

Some features save information in your browser's own storage (local storage and IndexedDB, which are not cookies). Each item is either something you asked the site to keep, or a record of a choice you made on screen. Nothing is stored to measure you, count your visits or recognize you, and none of it is sent to us.

| What | Where | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Your tracker entries, treatments, saved tool answers, doctor-report questions and reminder settings | IndexedDB (`menowell_local_db`) | You entered them and asked the app to keep them |
| Symptoms you pinned | Local storage (`menowell_pinned_symptoms`) | You pinned them in the checklist or onboarding |
| Pages you saved for later | Local storage (`menowell_bookmarks`) | You pressed "Save for later" |
| That you finished onboarding | Local storage (`menowell_onboarding_completed`) | A choice you made, so the intro isn't shown again |
| That you have used a tool | Local storage (`menowell_tool_used`) | So the offer to install the app only appears after you've used it, never on first visit |
| That you closed the privacy notice | Local storage (`menoverdict_notice_dismissed`) | A choice you made, so it isn't shown again |

You can delete all of it at any time from the app's settings, or by clearing this site's data in your browser.

## Other websites

When you click a link to a retailer or telehealth service, including our labeled affiliate links, that company may set its own cookies on its own site. Their cookie and privacy policies apply there. Read our [affiliate disclosure](/about/how-we-make-money/).

See also our [privacy policy](/privacy/).
