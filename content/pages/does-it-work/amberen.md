**Our verdict:** :grade{id="amberen"}. Two 90-day trials paid for by the maker found Amberen eased more menopause symptoms than a placebo, but no independent trial has checked the results [@radzinsky-amberen-pooled-trials-2019]. Hormone therapy is the most effective treatment for hot flashes [@nams-2022-hormone-therapy].

## What's in Amberen?

The formula tested in the published trials was two capsules taken once a day with a meal [@radzinsky-amberen-pooled-trials-2019]. They contained:

- **Capsule one**: 200 mg of ammonium succinate, a salt of succinic acid [@radzinsky-amberen-pooled-trials-2019].
- **Capsule two**: 200 mg of calcium disuccinate, plus monosodium L-glutamate, glycine, magnesium disuccinate, zinc difumarate and vitamin E (tocopherol acetate) [@radzinsky-amberen-pooled-trials-2019].

Today the company sells several products under the Amberen name, including menopause capsules and gummies, a perimenopause formula, and products for hot flashes, libido, focus and joints [@amberen-own-site-2026]. Its menopause capsule page describes the formula as a "proprietary mineral complex" of succinates, amino acids and vitamin E, but doesn't list amounts [@amberen-own-site-2026]. We can't confirm that any current product matches the one tested, so check the label against the list above.

## Does Amberen work? What the trials found

A 2019 paper pooled two identical randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled trials run in Russia. They included 227 women aged 42 to 60 who were past menopause and had mild to moderate symptoms (114 on Amberen, 113 on placebo) [@radzinsky-amberen-pooled-trials-2019]. After 90 days, compared with placebo, the Amberen group had:

- **Broader symptom relief**: Significant improvement in 16 of 21 menopause symptoms on a standard checklist, the Greene Climacteric Scale [@radzinsky-amberen-pooled-trials-2019].
- **Less anxiety**: A significant drop on a standard anxiety questionnaire [@radzinsky-amberen-pooled-trials-2019].
- **Weight and body size**: Lower weight, body mass index (BMI) and waist and hip size [@radzinsky-amberen-pooled-trials-2019].
- **Higher estradiol**: Blood levels of estradiol, a form of estrogen, rose from where they started [@radzinsky-amberen-pooled-trials-2019].

The authors note the limits themselves: the women were a fairly similar group, the trials were short, and they tell us nothing about long-term use [@radzinsky-amberen-pooled-trials-2019].

## Who paid for the research

The manufacturer funded both trials, including the capsules and placebo. One of the four authors is a paid consultant for the company, and the other three ran the trials [@radzinsky-amberen-pooled-trials-2019]. Randomizing, blinding and using a placebo all guard against bias, and these trials did all three. But no independent team has repeated the trials, which is why the grade stays at :grade{id="amberen"}.

## Amberen side effects and safety

No side effects were reported in the trials. There were no serious side effects in either group, women reported no side effects, and blood tests, urine tests and vital signs did not differ from placebo over 90 days [@radzinsky-amberen-pooled-trials-2019]. Some things are still unknown:

- **Long-term use**: The trials lasted 90 days, so they say nothing about longer use [@radzinsky-amberen-pooled-trials-2019].
- **People with health conditions**: The trials left out women with cancer, hormone (endocrine) conditions or psychiatric illness, and women who had recently used hormone therapy [@radzinsky-amberen-pooled-trials-2019]. Safety in those groups hasn't been tested.
- **Estrogen-sensitive conditions**: Because estradiol rose in users [@radzinsky-amberen-pooled-trials-2019], ask your doctor before taking it if you've had breast cancer or another hormone-sensitive condition.
- **No FDA review before sale**: By law, the FDA does not approve dietary supplements or their labels before they're sold; the company is responsible for safety and for backing up its claims [@fda-dietary-supplements-qa-2026].

## Does Amberen help with weight loss?

The evidence is mixed. In the pooled trials, women on Amberen lost slightly more weight and waist and hip size than women on placebo [@radzinsky-amberen-pooled-trials-2019]. But in 2015 the US Federal Trade Commission (FTC) charged its marketers with making unsupported weight-loss claims, saying a later study found no significant weight-loss difference between the test and control groups [@ftc-amberen-complaint-2015].

In 2016 the marketers settled FTC charges that they lacked support for claims that Amberen causes substantial weight loss and relieves nearly every common menopause symptom, and that they falsely said clinical studies proved these benefits [@ftc-amberen-settlement-2016]. The order bars such claims for any of their supplements unless they are backed by suitable human clinical testing [@ftc-amberen-settlement-2016].

## What do Amberen reviews tell you?

Star ratings and testimonials can't show whether Amberen works better than a placebo. Hot flashes often ease on a placebo too: in one large review of plant estrogen (phytoestrogen) supplements, they dropped by 1% to 59% in placebo groups [@lethaby-cochrane-phytoestrogens-2013]. The FTC also alleged that the company hid its ties to people endorsing the product and falsely claimed a 93 percent satisfaction rate [@ftc-amberen-complaint-2015; @ftc-amberen-settlement-2016].

The company's site cites a 90-day placebo-controlled study of 125 women, but doesn't name where it was published or who paid for it [@amberen-own-site-2026]. The pooled trials above are the published evidence we could check.

## What has stronger evidence for hot flashes?

Hormone therapy is the most effective treatment for hot flashes [@nams-2022-hormone-therapy]. Without hormones, The Menopause Society recommends CBT, clinical hypnosis, certain antidepressants (SSRIs and SNRIs), gabapentin and fezolinetant (Level I) [@nams-2023-nonhormone-therapy].
