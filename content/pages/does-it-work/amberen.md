**Our verdict: Limited evidence — real placebo-controlled trial data, but not yet independently replicated, and the published research discloses a financial tie to the manufacturer.**

Amberen's original formula, a succinate-based dietary supplement, is one of the few menopause supplements on this site's list that has actual randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial data behind it. That's genuinely notable — but it comes with an important caveat about who ran the research.

## What the trials actually found

A pooled analysis combined data from **two identical randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled 90-day trials** (114 women on the supplement, 113 on placebo, ages 42–60) [@radzinsky-amberen-pooled-trials-2019]. Compared with placebo, the supplement group showed:

- **Significant improvement in 16 of 21 menopausal symptoms** measured on the Greene Climacteric Scale (p≤0.05)
- A **significant reduction in anxiety** (p<0.0001, State-Trait Anxiety Inventory)
- **Significant reductions in weight, BMI, and waist/hip circumference**
- A **significant increase in serum estradiol** versus baseline

That's a meaningfully positive, well-designed trial result [@radzinsky-amberen-pooled-trials-2019].

## The conflict of interest worth knowing about

Here's the caveat we think matters most: the published paper's own conflict-of-interest disclosure states that one of the four authors **is a paid consultant for the manufacturer** of the supplement being studied, and the other three authors were **investigators** on the trials — meaning none of the four authors were independent of the research they were reporting on [@radzinsky-amberen-pooled-trials-2019].

This doesn't mean the results are false. Randomization, double-blinding, and placebo control are real safeguards against bias, and this trial used all three. But industry-sponsored and industry-linked research has a well-documented pattern of producing more favorable results than independent studies asking the same question — which is exactly why **independent replication** matters before a grade higher than Limited is warranted. We haven't found an independent, non-industry-linked trial of this product to point to yet.

## One more thing worth flagging

Amberen's current product lineup has expanded well beyond the original formula tested in these trials — into separate products for libido, joint support ("Keep Agile"), focus, and more. The research discussed here is specific to the **original succinate-based menopause formula**; we haven't found trial data covering these newer, separate product lines, so this verdict doesn't extend to them.

## What to consider

- **Real placebo-controlled trial data exists** for the original formula — a step up from most competitors on this list
- **Industry ties mean independent replication is still needed** before we'd grade this higher
- **Newer product-line extensions** (libido, joint, focus formulas) aren't covered by this research
