**Our verdict: Not recommended as a treatment for hot flashes (Level II).**

Cannabis and CBD (cannabidiol) products are increasingly marketed for perimenopause and menopause symptoms, including [hot flashes](/symptoms/hot-flashes/), sleep, and mood. After reviewing the randomized trial literature in its **2023 Nonhormone Therapy Position Statement**, The Menopause Society placed **cannabinoids** in the **not recommended** category for vasomotor symptoms (Level II) [@nams-2023-nonhormone-therapy].

## Two separate problems: evidence, and product quality

This verdict covers effectiveness for hot flashes specifically — the trial evidence doesn't support cannabinoids as a reliable treatment for vasomotor symptoms [@nams-2023-nonhormone-therapy]. There's a second, practical issue worth knowing about too: cannabis and CBD products are largely **unregulated for purity and dosing accuracy** in most markets, meaning what's on the label doesn't always match what's in the product. They can also **interact with other medications** you may be taking.

Together, that's an unproven benefit combined with real variability in what you're actually getting — worth weighing carefully before spending money on a product marketed this way.

## What actually has evidence behind it

- **[Hormone therapy](/treatments/hormone-therapy/)**: Most effective treatment for vasomotor symptoms without contraindications [@nams-2022-hormone-therapy].
- **[CBT and clinical hypnosis](/treatments/nonhormonal/cbt-and-hypnosis/)**: Level I, with real benefits for both mood and sleep [@nams-2023-nonhormone-therapy].
- **[Non-hormonal prescriptions](/treatments/nonhormonal/)**: SSRIs/SNRIs, gabapentin, NK3 antagonists, all Level I [@nams-2023-nonhormone-therapy].
