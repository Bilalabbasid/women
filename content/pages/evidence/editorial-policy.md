**MenoVerdict** publishes independent, evidence-graded information for women in perimenopause, menopause, and postmenopause. Every article, tool, and guide follows our [evidence evaluation standards](/evidence/) for source verification and medical honesty.

## 1. Source hierarchy and verification

We never invent studies, statistics, DOIs, quotes, or clinical claims. Before any medical statement is included on a page, our editorial team opens the primary source and records its exact finding in our public source registry. We weight evidence in this order:

1. **Regulatory prescribing labels and safety communications** (such as U.S. FDA prescribing information and UK MHRA notices).
2. **Clinical society practice guidelines and consensus statements** (including The Menopause Society, NICE NG23 in the UK, ACOG, and the International Menopause Society).
3. **Systematic reviews and large randomized controlled trials (RCTs)** (such as Cochrane reviews and trials run at many clinics).
4. **Smaller trials and observational studies**, which we explicitly label as limited evidence.

See our [evidence grading method](/evidence/how-we-grade/) for how these sources translate into the badge shown at the top of each medical article.

## 2. Medical review honesty

We never display a "medically reviewed" badge, a clinician's name, or medical reviewer schema unless a verified, licensed clinician has reviewed and approved that exact version of the page. When a page is edited, that approval is removed automatically until a clinician reviews the new version.

Until a page has been reviewed by a named clinician, it displays a clear notice stating that the content is general information based on published sources and was not written by a doctor.

## 3. No diagnosis or prescribing

Our articles and [interactive tools](/tools/) explain possible causes, clinical guidelines, and questions to take to an appointment. They never diagnose a condition, tell you which stage of menopause you are in as a medical fact, recommend that you take a specific drug, or give personalized medication doses.

## 4. Independence

MenoVerdict carries no ads, affiliate links or sponsored content. See [how MenoVerdict is funded](/about/how-we-make-money/).

## 5. Corrections and updates

We review articles when regulatory labels or clinical guidelines change, and we log every material update on our [corrections and updates page](/evidence/updates/) as well as in the change log at the bottom of each article. If you spot an error, please reach out through our [contact page](/contact/).
