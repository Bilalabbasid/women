Every medical claim on this site links to a numbered reference at the bottom of its page. This page collects all of those references in one place — every source currently in our registry, generated directly from the file we use to build the site, not copied by hand.

If you want to check a specific article's citations, open that article and scroll to its References section. This page is for seeing everything at once, or for pulling the data yourself.

## How to read this list

Sources are grouped the way our [evidence grading method](/evidence/how-we-grade/) orders them: regulator prescribing labels and statements first, then society and clinical guidelines, then systematic reviews and randomized controlled trials, then cohort studies and other sources, with small studies, preprints, and brand-funded research called out separately so you can weigh them accordingly. Within each group, newest first.

Each entry links to the original source (its publisher page or DOI) and lists which published pages on this site currently cite it. A source with a funding conflict shows it directly, not in fine print elsewhere.

::sources-list

## Using this data yourself

The same registry is available as structured JSON at [/sources.json](/sources.json), generated from the same file as this page, for anyone who wants to check our citations programmatically rather than by reading this list.

## Something missing or wrong?

If a source here no longer resolves, or you think we've misread what it says, please [contact our editorial team](/contact/). See our [corrections and updates log](/evidence/updates/) for how we handle fixes.
