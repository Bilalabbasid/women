This page lists the regulatory actions, guideline changes and corrections that changed what this site says. Each entry names its source and the pages it affected. The newest is first.

## 2026

### February 12, 2026: FDA approves label changes for six hormone therapy products
- **What changed**: The FDA approved revised labels for six menopausal hormone therapy products, removing the cardiovascular disease, breast cancer and probable dementia statements from the boxed warning [@fda-mht-labeling-changes-2026].
- **Pages updated**: [The FDA HRT boxed warning change, explained](/treatments/hormone-therapy/boxed-warning/), [Menopause treatment options](/treatments/) and [Hot flashes in perimenopause](/symptoms/hot-flashes/).

## 2025

### November 10, 2025: FDA asks makers to change hormone therapy labels
- **What changed**: The FDA asked manufacturers to remove most boxed-warning language and the "lowest effective dose for the shortest time" recommendation, and to add starting therapy before age 60 or within 10 years of menopause as a consideration for moderate to severe hot flashes [@fda-mht-labeling-request-2025].
- **Pages updated**: The regulatory timeline in [The FDA HRT boxed warning change, explained](/treatments/hormone-therapy/boxed-warning/).
