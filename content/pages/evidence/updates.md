Science and regulatory guidance evolve continuously. When health authorities release new prescribing guidance, clinical societies update treatment recommendations, or our editorial team corrects an error, we log every change here.

## 2026 Updates

### February 12, 2026: FDA Approves Labeling Changes on Six MHT Formulations
- **Action**: FDA approved revised prescribing labels for six menopausal hormone therapy products, removing cardiovascular and dementia statements from the prominent boxed warning.
- **Affected articles**: Updated [The FDA HRT boxed warning change, explained](/treatments/hormone-therapy/boxed-warning/), [Menopause treatment options](/treatments/), and [Hot flashes in perimenopause](/symptoms/hot-flashes/).
- **Clinical impact**: Strengthens guidance that initiating therapy within 10 years of menopause or before age 60 provides favorable benefit-risk balance for moderate to severe vasomotor symptoms.

## 2025 Updates

### November 10, 2025: FDA Safety Request to Hormone Manufacturers
- **Action**: FDA formally requested manufacturers update prescribing labels and eliminate the &ldquo;lowest dose for shortest duration&rdquo; restriction.
- **Affected articles**: Added to the regulatory timeline in our hormone therapy guides.

### May 2025: Non-hormonal NK3 Receptor Guidelines
- **Action**: The Menopause Society issued updated clinical consensus recognizing neurokinin 3 receptor antagonists as effective first-line non-hormonal prescription options for vasomotor symptoms.
- **Affected articles**: Integrated into [Hot flashes](/symptoms/hot-flashes/) and [Night sweats](/symptoms/night-sweats/).

## Submitting a correction

If you spot a factual error, broken link, or outdated citation in any article, please email our editorial team. Material corrections are updated within 48 hours and documented in the relevant article&apos;s change log.
