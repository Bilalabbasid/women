Hot flashes, night sweats and vaginal dryness are common after breast cancer, and treatments such as chemotherapy, tamoxifen and aromatase inhibitors can cause them [@acs-mht-after-breast-cancer-2025]. Doctors generally don't recommend hormone therapy (HRT) after breast cancer [@acs-mht-after-breast-cancer-2025; @nice-ng23-recommendations]. Talking therapy (CBT), some prescription medicines and vaginal moisturizers have good evidence instead [@nams-2023-nonhormone-therapy; @nams-2020-gsm].

## Can you take HRT after breast cancer?

Usually not. The American Cancer Society says doctors generally don't recommend menopausal hormone therapy after breast cancer, because many breast cancers grow in response to estrogen [@acs-mht-after-breast-cancer-2025]. In a randomized trial (HABITS), survivors taking hormone therapy were much more likely to have their cancer come back or a new breast cancer [@acs-mht-after-breast-cancer-2025].

How firm that advice is depends on your cancer [@acs-mht-after-breast-cancer-2025]:

- **Hormone receptor-positive (ER+ or PR+) cancer**: The risk with hormone therapy is clearly higher, so it is typically not recommended.
- **Hormone receptor-negative (ER- and PR-) cancer**: The risk is less clear. Some doctors might consider hormone therapy if non-hormonal treatments aren't helping.

UK NICE guidance also says to stop systemic HRT when breast cancer is diagnosed [@nice-ng23-recommendations]. Any hormone use after breast cancer is a decision to make with your oncology team [@nams-2022-hormone-therapy]. For background on risk in general, see [HRT and breast cancer risk](/treatments/hormone-therapy/breast-cancer-risk/).

## What causes menopause symptoms after breast cancer?

Chemotherapy can bring on menopause symptoms if you hadn't been through menopause yet, and tamoxifen, aromatase inhibitors and other hormone-blocking medicines can also cause them [@acs-mht-after-breast-cancer-2025; @medlineplus-cancer-hot-flashes-night-sweats]. If you were already past menopause, symptoms can return when you stop HRT at diagnosis [@acs-mht-after-breast-cancer-2025].

The most common ones are [hot flashes](/symptoms/hot-flashes/), [night sweats](/symptoms/night-sweats/) and [vaginal dryness](/symptoms/vaginal-dryness/). If chemotherapy stopped your periods, see [chemotherapy-induced menopause](/groups/chemotherapy-induced-menopause/).

## What helps hot flashes after breast cancer?

Several non-hormonal treatments reduced how often hot flashes happened, or how much they bothered people, in randomized trials, according to The Menopause Society's 2023 statement [@nams-2023-nonhormone-therapy]. These are the options:

- **CBT and clinical hypnosis** (:grade{id="cbt-hypnosis-vms"}): Reduce how much hot flashes and night sweats disrupt daily life and sleep, and involve no medicine [@nams-2023-nonhormone-therapy].
- **Some antidepressants (SSRIs/SNRIs), gabapentin and oxybutynin**: Prescription medicines shown to reduce hot flashes [@nams-2023-nonhormone-therapy]. The American Cancer Society also lists clonidine, a blood pressure medicine [@acs-mht-after-breast-cancer-2025].
- **NK3 blockers** (:grade{id="nk3-vms"}): Non-hormonal medicines such as [fezolinetant (Veozah)](/treatments/nonhormonal/fezolinetant/) [@nams-2023-nonhormone-therapy]. The American Cancer Society lists fezolinetant and [elinzanetant (Lynkuet)](/treatments/nonhormonal/elinzanetant/) among the options [@acs-mht-after-breast-cancer-2025]. Fezolinetant's label warns about liver injury and requires liver blood tests before starting and during the first months [@veozah-prescribing-information].

### Which antidepressants are safer with tamoxifen?

Some SSRIs, especially paroxetine and fluoxetine, can block the enzyme (CYP2D6) your body uses to turn tamoxifen into its most active form. The Menopause Society names venlafaxine, desvenlafaxine, escitalopram or citalopram as safer choices with tamoxifen [@nams-2023-nonhormone-therapy], so your oncologist and prescriber should choose together. See [SSRIs and SNRIs for hot flashes](/treatments/nonhormonal/ssris-snris/).

## Are soy and herbal supplements safe after breast cancer?

Eating soy foods seems to be safe for breast cancer survivors, but it isn't clear that concentrated supplements are [@acs-mht-after-breast-cancer-2025]. The American Cancer Society says it is not clear that phytoestrogen supplements, such as soy, red clover or black cohosh, are safe after hormone receptor-positive cancer [@acs-mht-after-breast-cancer-2025].

Over-the-counter herbal remedies and [menopause supplements](/does-it-work/) are also **not recommended** by The Menopause Society for hot flashes (:grade{id="otc-supplements-vms"}) [@nams-2023-nonhormone-therapy]. UK NICE guidance warns that St John's wort can interact with medicines including tamoxifen [@nice-ng23-recommendations].

## Is vaginal estrogen safe after breast cancer?

It isn't the first step, and it is a decision to make with your oncologist. The Menopause Society (2020) and ACOG (2021) both recommend **regular vaginal moisturizers** (used several times a week) plus **lubricants** during sex first [@nams-2020-gsm; @acog-clinical-consensus-gsm]. NICE says the same for people with a history of breast cancer [@nice-ng23-recommendations].

If symptoms continue:

- **NICE**: Low-dose vaginal estrogen can be considered, and people taking aromatase inhibitors should decide this with a breast cancer specialist [@nice-ng23-recommendations].
- **The Menopause Society**: There isn't enough data yet to confirm vaginal estrogen is safe after breast cancer, so it should be prescribed together with your oncologist [@nams-2020-gsm; @nams-2022-hormone-therapy].
- **American Cancer Society**: Low-dose vaginal hormone products might help if moisturizers and lubricants don't, after talking through the risks and benefits with your care team [@acs-mht-after-breast-cancer-2025].

Read more about [vaginal estrogen](/treatments/hormone-therapy/vaginal-estrogen/).

## When to see a doctor

- **Liver warning signs on fezolinetant**: The label warns of liver injury, so contact your prescriber promptly if you have signs such as yellow skin or eyes, dark urine, or pain in the upper right belly [@veozah-prescribing-information].
- **Any vaginal bleeding after menopause**: Get it checked by a doctor, even if it's light or happens once [@nhs-postmenopausal-bleeding]. See [bleeding after menopause](/postmenopause/bleeding/).
- **Symptoms that disrupt your sleep, work, or relationships**: There are proven options to try, so ask your oncology or menopause team rather than waiting it out [@nams-2023-nonhormone-therapy].
