**Premature ovarian insufficiency (POI)** occurs when ovarian function declines before **age 40**, causing infrequent or absent periods (oligomenorrhoea or amenorrhoea) and low estrogen levels [@nice-ng23]. Because POI happens years before the average age of natural menopause, both diagnosis and treatment follow specialist rules that differ from standard midlife [perimenopause](/perimenopause/) guidance [@nice-ng23; @nice-ng23-recommendations].

## How clinicians diagnose POI (unlike menopause at 45+)

Whereas NICE guideline NG23 advises against routine hormone blood tests at age 45 and over [@nice-ng23-recommendations], **under age 40 laboratory testing is essential** [@nice-ng23]. Clinicians assess both your menstrual history (such as 4 or more months of absent or irregular periods) and elevated follicle-stimulating hormone (FSH) levels on two blood samples taken weeks apart, while ruling out pregnancy and other endocrine causes [@nice-ng23]. Read more in [Is there a blood test for perimenopause?](/perimenopause/blood-tests/).

## Protecting bone, heart, and symptom health

Without hormone replacement, years of early estrogen deficiency increase the risk of rapid bone mineral loss ([osteoporosis](/postmenopause/bone-health/)) and cardiovascular disease [@nams-2022-hormone-therapy]. Unless there is a specific medical contraindication (such as a hormone-sensitive cancer), guidelines recommend continuing hormone replacement at least until the average age of natural menopause [@nice-ng23; @nams-2022-hormone-therapy]. Check our [Does It Work? evidence directory](/does-it-work/) for what trial evidence says about exercise and non-hormonal support alongside medical care.

## Emotional support and when to get help now

Being told in your 20s or 30s that your ovaries are not working normally — especially when fertility is affected — can feel overwhelming. Ask your clinician for a referral to a specialist POI clinic and psychological support [@nice-ng23].

:::urgent
- **If you are feeling overwhelmed or having thoughts of harming yourself:** call or text **988** in the US [@lifeline-988] or call **116 123** (Samaritans) in the UK [@samaritans-contact], free and confidential 24/7.
:::
