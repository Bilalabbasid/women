How long menopause lasts depends on what you count: menopause itself is one point, 12 months after your last period, but its symptoms usually last 7 to 9 years [@medlineplus-menopause-2026; @nhs-menopause-symptoms]. Frequent hot flashes last a median of 7.4 years [@swan-vms-duration-avis-2015]. Hormone therapy is the most effective treatment while they last [@nams-2022-hormone-therapy].

## How long does menopause last?

Menopause is a single moment: the point when you've gone 12 months without a period [@medlineplus-menopause-2026]. What people usually mean by "menopause" is the whole stretch around it, which takes years. That stretch has three stages, and each one has its own timeline.

| Stage | What it is | How long it lasts |
|---|---|---|
| [Perimenopause](/perimenopause/how-long/) | From the first cycle changes to 12 months after the last period [@straw-10-2012] | About 4 years on average, up to 10 for some [@owh-menopause-basics-2026] |
| Menopause | The point 12 months after your last period [@medlineplus-menopause-2026] | A single day, looking back [@medlineplus-menopause-2026] |
| Early postmenopause | The years after menopause, while hormone levels settle [@straw-10-2012] | About 5 to 8 years [@straw-10-2012] |
| [Postmenopause](/postmenopause/) | Everything after menopause [@cleveland-clinic-postmenopause-2024] | The rest of your life [@cleveland-clinic-postmenopause-2024] |

Most people reach menopause between ages 45 and 55 [@medlineplus-menopause-2026]. The US National Institute on Aging says the transition may last several years [@nia-what-is-menopause].

## How long do menopause symptoms last?

Menopause symptoms usually last 7 to 9 years, sometimes longer, according to the NHS, and they can change along the way [@nhs-menopause-symptoms]. For example, hot flashes may ease and be replaced by low mood or anxiety [@nhs-menopause-symptoms]. The US National Institute on Aging gives a range of 2 to 8 years and stresses that timing varies a lot [@nia-what-is-menopause].

Figures differ because sources count different things: some count all symptoms, others only frequent hot flashes. UK NICE guidance sums it up: symptoms range from mild to severe and can last for short or long periods [@nice-ng23-recommendations]. Each symptom also follows its own timeline.

| Symptom | How long it tends to last |
|---|---|
| Hot flashes and night sweats | Median 7.4 years in total; median 4.5 years after the final period [@swan-vms-duration-avis-2015] |
| Vaginal dryness and painful sex | Can continue after menopause [@nhs-menopause-symptoms] and become more common in later postmenopause [@straw-10-2012] |
| Low mood and anxiety | Can appear as hot flashes ease [@nhs-menopause-symptoms] |
| Joint pain | Can continue after menopause [@nhs-menopause-symptoms] |
| Body fat gain | Faster from the start of the transition until about 2 years after the final period, then levels off [@greendale-swan-body-composition-2019] |

## How long do hot flashes last?

A single hot flash (hot flush in the UK) usually lasts between 30 seconds and 10 minutes [@nia-what-is-menopause]. Over the years, frequent hot flashes and night sweats lasted a median of 7.4 years in total in SWAN, a US study that followed more than 3,300 women for up to 17 years [@swan-vms-duration-avis-2015]. The US Office on Women's Health puts the average at 7 to 12 years, and longer for some women [@owh-menopause-basics-2026].

When they start matters. Women whose frequent hot flashes began while their periods were still regular or only starting to change had the longest course, a median of more than 11.8 years [@swan-vms-duration-avis-2015]. Women whose hot flashes began only after their final period had the shortest, a median of 3.4 years [@swan-vms-duration-avis-2015]. See [hot flashes](/symptoms/hot-flashes/) for what they feel like and how to treat them.

### How long do hot flashes last after menopause?

Hot flashes often continue for about 4 to 5 years after the final period. In SWAN, frequent hot flashes lasted a median of 4.5 years after it [@swan-vms-duration-avis-2015]. In the Penn Ovarian Aging Study, moderate or severe hot flashes peaked in the first 2 years after the final period and then lasted a mean of 4.6 years after it [@freeman-poas-long-term-hot-flashes-2014].

They can last much longer for some. One third of the Penn women observed for 10 years or more after menopause still had moderate or severe hot flashes [@freeman-poas-long-term-hot-flashes-2014]. In an Australian survey, 6.5% of women aged 60 to 65 still had moderate to severe hot flashes or night sweats [@gartoulla-vms-women-60-65-2015].

### What makes hot flashes last longer?

Studies have linked longer-lasting hot flashes with these factors:

- **Starting early**: Hot flashes that began before or early in perimenopause lasted longest [@swan-vms-duration-avis-2015].
- **Race and ethnicity**: African American women had the longest median duration in SWAN, 10.1 years [@swan-vms-duration-avis-2015]. In the Penn study, African American women and obese white women had higher risks of hot flashes after menopause [@freeman-poas-long-term-hot-flashes-2014].
- **Stress, anxiety and low mood**: Greater perceived stress and more depressive and anxiety symptoms when hot flashes began were linked to a longer course [@swan-vms-duration-avis-2015].
- **Smoking and weight**: Smoking and being overweight were linked to more moderate to severe hot flashes in the Australian survey [@gartoulla-vms-women-60-65-2015].

These are links seen in studies, not proof that changing one factor will shorten your hot flashes.

## How long does vaginal dryness last?

Vaginal dryness usually doesn't fade the way hot flashes do. It's one of the symptoms that can continue after menopause [@nhs-menopause-symptoms], and it becomes more common in the later years after menopause [@straw-10-2012]. Lower estrogen makes vaginal tissue thinner and drier [@cleveland-clinic-postmenopause-2024], which can affect sex and comfort (see [vaginal dryness](/symptoms/vaginal-dryness/)).

The good news is that it's very treatable. Guidelines recommend regular non-hormonal vaginal moisturizers and lubricants first, and low-dose vaginal estrogen (oestrogen in the UK) when moderate to severe symptoms persist [@nams-2020-gsm; @acog-clinical-consensus-gsm].

## How long does menopause weight gain last?

The shift toward more body fat seems to settle about 2 years after the final period. In SWAN, the rate of fat gain doubled when the menopause transition began and continued until about 2 years after the final period, then leveled off [@greendale-swan-body-composition-2019]. Weight itself rose steadily before the transition and flattened afterward [@greendale-swan-body-composition-2019]. See [menopause weight gain](/symptoms/weight-gain/) for what helps.

## How long does menopause last after a hysterectomy?

It depends on whether your ovaries were removed. Removing only the uterus stops your periods but doesn't cause menopause, and you typically won't have menopause symptoms earlier than expected [@medlineplus-menopause-2026; @cleveland-clinic-menopause-after-hysterectomy-2025]. Without periods to track, it's harder to know when that happens [@straw-10-2012].

If both ovaries are removed, the surgery brings on menopause [@medlineplus-menopause-2026]. Symptoms may begin very soon after surgery and may be more severe than in natural menopause [@cleveland-clinic-menopause-after-hysterectomy-2025]. How long they last varies, and doctors can't predict it for one person [@cleveland-clinic-menopause-after-hysterectomy-2025]. See [surgical menopause](/groups/surgical-menopause/) and [hysterectomy with ovaries kept](/groups/hysterectomy-ovaries-retained/).

## What helps while symptoms last?

You don't have to wait symptoms out. These options have the best evidence:

- **Hormone therapy (HRT)**: :grade{id="mht-vms"}. The most effective treatment for hot flashes and for vaginal symptoms [@nams-2022-hormone-therapy]. See [hormone therapy](/treatments/hormone-therapy/).
- **Non-hormonal prescription medicines**: :grade{id="nonhormonal-rx-vms"}. Options such as low-dose SSRIs or SNRIs, gabapentin and fezolinetant reduce hot flashes [@nams-2023-nonhormone-therapy]. See [non-hormonal treatments](/treatments/nonhormonal/).
- **CBT and clinical hypnosis**: :grade{id="cbt-hypnosis-vms"}. Talking and hypnosis therapies, without medicine, that can reduce how much hot flashes bother you [@nams-2023-nonhormone-therapy; @owh-menopause-treatment-2026].
- **Vaginal moisturizers and lubricants**: :grade{id="vaginal-moisturizers-gsm"}. First-line care for vaginal dryness [@nams-2020-gsm].
- **Low-dose vaginal estrogen**: :grade{id="vaginal-estrogen-gsm"}. For dryness or painful sex that persists [@nams-2020-gsm].

## When to see a doctor

See a doctor (your GP in the UK) if symptoms are affecting your sleep, work or daily life, or you want to know your options; effective treatments exist [@nams-2022-hormone-therapy; @nhs-menopause-symptoms].

:::urgent
- **Any vaginal bleeding or spotting after 12 months without a period** should be checked by a doctor, even a small amount or a single episode [@nhs-postmenopausal-bleeding]. See [bleeding after menopause](/postmenopause/bleeding/).
:::
