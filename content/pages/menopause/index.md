In everyday conversation, "menopause" is often used for the whole midlife hormonal transition. In clinical medicine and research, **menopause** refers specifically to the permanent end of menstrual periods — confirmed once you have gone **12 consecutive months without a period** (when not using hormonal contraception) [@straw-10-2012; @nice-ng23-recommendations].

The years leading up to that 12-month mark are called [perimenopause](/perimenopause/), and the years after it are [postmenopause](/postmenopause/). For a side-by-side comparison of the two stages, see [Perimenopause vs menopause: what is the difference?](/perimenopause/vs-menopause/).

## What happens in the body at menopause

As the ovarian reserve of follicles reaches depletion, ovarian production of estradiol and progesterone falls permanently, and menstrual cycling stops [@straw-10-2012]. According to NICE guideline NG23, menopause-associated symptoms can affect multiple systems and range from mild to severe over short or long periods [@nice-ng23-recommendations]:

- **Vasomotor symptoms:** [Hot flashes](/symptoms/hot-flashes/) and [night sweats](/symptoms/night-sweats/)
- **Genitourinary syndrome of menopause (GSM):** [Vaginal dryness](/symptoms/vaginal-dryness/), [painful sex](/symptoms/painful-sex/), and urinary changes such as [incontinence](/symptoms/incontinence/)
- **Sleep and mood:** [Insomnia](/symptoms/insomnia/), [anxiety](/symptoms/anxiety/), and [brain fog](/symptoms/brain-fog/)
- **Musculoskeletal and metabolic shifts:** [Joint and muscle pain](/symptoms/joint-pain/) and [bone mineral density loss](/postmenopause/bone-health/)

## Evidence-graded treatment options

The Menopause Society states that [menopausal hormone therapy (HRT)](/treatments/hormone-therapy/) is the most effective treatment for hot flashes, night sweats, and genitourinary syndrome of menopause, and prevents bone loss and fracture, with a favorable benefit-risk ratio for healthy women under age 60 or within 10 years of menopause onset [@nams-2022-hormone-therapy]. For women who cannot or prefer not to use hormones, proven [non-hormonal treatments](/treatments/nonhormonal/) and [evidence-graded lifestyle strategies](/does-it-work/exercise/) are available.
