If you are 45 or older and experiencing [early signs of perimenopause](/perimenopause/early-signs/) — such as shifting cycles alongside new hot flashes or night sweats — clinical guidelines state that you usually **do not need a hormone blood test** to confirm [perimenopause](/perimenopause/) [@nice-ng23-recommendations].

## What NICE guideline NG23 advises at age 45 and over

In the UK, NICE guideline NG23 (Recommendations 1.3.1 and 1.3.4) gives explicit instructions on laboratory testing [@nice-ng23-recommendations]:

- **Identify without hormone blood tests at 45+:** In otherwise healthy people aged 45 or over, perimenopause is identified clinically when vasomotor symptoms have recently started together with a change in menstrual cycles [@nice-ng23-recommendations].
- **Tests not to use at 45+:** Do **not** use anti-Müllerian hormone (AMH), inhibin A or B, oestradiol, antral follicle count, or ovarian volume to identify perimenopause or menopause in this age group [@nice-ng23-recommendations].

Because ovarian hormone production fluctuates erratically during the menopausal transition [@straw-10-2012], a single estradiol or FSH draw can look completely normal one week and elevated the next. Note that a clinician may still order **other** blood tests (such as thyroid function or iron studies) to rule out non-menopausal causes of fatigue, heavy bleeding, or mood changes.

## When hormone blood tests *are* needed (under age 45)

Age changes the testing rule:

- **Under age 40:** If you have irregular or absent periods or menopausal symptoms before 40, blood tests are essential to assess for [premature ovarian insufficiency (POI)](/groups/premature-ovarian-insufficiency/) [@nice-ng23].
- **Ages 40 to 44:** When menopause-like symptoms begin between 40 and 44, clinicians may consider targeted testing alongside a full review of other possible causes [@nice-ng23].

## What to bring instead of a commercial test kit

Skip unverified over-the-counter tests or [supplement kits](/does-it-work/). Instead, use our [STRAW+10 perimenopause stage guide](/tools/stage-guide/) and bring two to four weeks of entries from your [private symptom tracker](/tools/symptom-tracker/) to your appointment.
