Many women in [perimenopause](/perimenopause/) describe a sudden, unfamiliar sense of dread, racing thoughts, or physical jitteriness — often waking them from sleep alongside [insomnia](/symptoms/insomnia/) or [night sweats](/symptoms/night-sweats/). UK NICE guideline NG23 explicitly lists effects on mood among the core menopause-associated symptoms [@nice-ng23-recommendations].

Because thyroid overactivity, iron deficiency, medication side effects, and [heart palpitations](/symptoms/heart-palpitations/) can mimic or worsen anxiety, a clinical check is important whenever new anxiety appears.

## What can help

- **Cognitive behavioral therapy (CBT):** Supported by Level I evidence in The Menopause Society's 2023 position statement for reducing vasomotor bother and helping with menopause-related mood and sleep distress [@nams-2023-nonhormone-therapy].
- **Treating underlying night sweats and sleep loss:** When nocturnal hot flashes fragment sleep night after night, daytime anxiety worsens. Both [hormone therapy](/treatments/hormone-therapy/) [@nams-2022-hormone-therapy] and [Level I non-hormonal options](/treatments/nonhormonal/) (including certain SSRIs/SNRIs that treat both anxiety and hot flashes [@nams-2023-nonhormone-therapy]) can be discussed with your clinician.
- **Skipping unproven botanicals:** Over-the-counter herbal blends and cannabinoids/CBD are not recommended by guidelines for menopausal symptoms (Levels I–II) [@nams-2023-nonhormone-therapy] — see our [Does It Work? evidence directory](/does-it-work/).

## When to get help now

:::urgent
- **If you are having thoughts of harming yourself or feel unable to stay safe:** call or text **988** in the US [@lifeline-988] or call **116 123** (Samaritans) in the UK [@samaritans-contact], free and confidential 24/7.
- **If anxiety comes with palpitations that do not go away, chest pain, shortness of breath, or feeling faint:** call 911 (US) or 999 (UK) or go to the emergency room [@nhs-heart-palpitations].
:::
