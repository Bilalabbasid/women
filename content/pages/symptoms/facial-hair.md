New coarse hairs on the chin, upper lip or jaw are common as periods wind down: about half of women past menopause have some facial hair, usually with normal hormone levels [@kamp-menopause-hair-disorders-2022]. Plucking, waxing, laser and a prescription cream can all help [@medlineplus-hirsutism; @dailymed-vaniqa-eflornithine]. Hair that grows fast or comes with a deeper voice needs a doctor's check [@medlineplus-hirsutism].

## Is new facial hair normal in perimenopause?

Yes, it's common and usually harmless. A 2022 dermatology review found facial hair in about 50% of women after menopause, mostly with normal androgen (male hormone) levels [@kamp-menopause-hair-disorders-2022]. Doctors call coarse, dark hair where women usually have fine hair, such as the upper lip and chin, hirsutism [@medlineplus-hirsutism].

Most of the time hirsutism doesn't cause health problems, though many women find it bothersome [@medlineplus-hirsutism].

## Why does facial hair grow in perimenopause?

As estrogen falls during the menopause transition, the balance tips toward androgens, even when androgen levels don't rise [@khunger-mehrotra-menopausal-acne-2019]. Researchers point to this imbalance between estrogen and testosterone, though they haven't fully worked out the link [@kamp-menopause-hair-disorders-2022].

The same shift can bring [adult acne](/symptoms/acne/) and [thinning hair on the scalp](/symptoms/hair-thinning/) [@khunger-mehrotra-menopausal-acne-2019]. Facial hair often runs in families too [@medlineplus-hirsutism].

## How can I get rid of facial hair?

Choose by how much hair there is and how long you want results to last:

| Option | How it works | Things to know |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Shaving** | Cuts hair at the surface [@medlineplus-hirsutism] | Doesn't make more hair grow, though it may look thicker [@medlineplus-hirsutism] |
| **Plucking, waxing or hair-removal creams** | Remove hair for a while; plucking and waxing are widely used [@medlineplus-hirsutism; @kamp-menopause-hair-disorders-2022] | Results are temporary [@medlineplus-hirsutism] |
| **Laser hair removal** | Targets the pigment in dark hair [@medlineplus-hirsutism] | Works best on dark hair; doesn't work on blond or red hair [@medlineplus-hirsutism] |
| **Electrolysis** | An electric current permanently damages each hair root [@medlineplus-hirsutism] | Expensive and needs several sessions [@medlineplus-hirsutism] |
| **Eflornithine 13.9% cream** (prescription) | Approved to reduce unwanted facial hair in women [@dailymed-vaniqa-eflornithine] | Improvement may show in 4 to 8 weeks; keep using your usual removal method; hair may return 8 weeks after stopping; can sting, burn or redden skin [@dailymed-vaniqa-eflornithine] |
| **Spironolactone** (prescription) | Blocks male hormones; one of the review's treatment options [@kamp-menopause-hair-disorders-2022] | Can take many months to notice less growth [@medlineplus-hirsutism] |

Weight loss may reduce hair growth in women who are overweight [@medlineplus-hirsutism].

## Can HRT or birth control help with facial hair?

Hormone therapy isn't an established treatment for it: the 2022 review found too little evidence to say HRT helps hair changes after menopause [@kamp-menopause-hair-disorders-2022]. Birth control pills are an option for some women with hirsutism [@medlineplus-hirsutism], but combined pills are usually avoided after menopause, partly because estrogen in older women raises concerns such as heart disease and blood clots [@khunger-mehrotra-menopausal-acne-2019].

If you're still having periods, see [birth control in perimenopause](/perimenopause/contraception/).

Some medicines can cause unwanted hair, including testosterone, DHEA, steroid medicines (glucocorticoids) and minoxidil, which some women use for thinning hair [@medlineplus-hirsutism]. If you take or are thinking about [testosterone](/treatments/hormone-therapy/testosterone/), mention any new facial hair to your doctor.

## Will menopause facial hair go away?

We don't know: no study has followed facial hair after menopause to see whether it fades. What is known is how long treatments last. Electrolysis permanently damages the hair roots it treats [@medlineplus-hirsutism], while eflornithine works only while you use it, with hair returning about 8 weeks after stopping [@dailymed-vaniqa-eflornithine].

## When to see a doctor about facial hair

Most new chin or lip hair doesn't need tests, but a doctor may check your testosterone level to rule out high androgens [@kamp-menopause-hair-disorders-2022]. If you're still having periods and they're irregular, together with acne and obesity, that can point to polycystic ovary syndrome (PCOS) [@kamp-menopause-hair-disorders-2022]. Also mention it if you think a medicine is causing new hair growth [@medlineplus-hirsutism].

:::urgent
See a doctor soon if hair grows quickly, or if it comes with acne, a deeper voice, more muscle, male-pattern thinning on your scalp, an enlarging clitoris or smaller breasts [@medlineplus-hirsutism]. Sudden or severe hair growth needs checking for a hormone-making tumor, a less common cause [@kamp-menopause-hair-disorders-2022; @medlineplus-hirsutism].
:::
