A short fuse, a loss of patience, or sudden flashes of anger that feel out of character are a common complaint in [perimenopause](/perimenopause/). NICE guideline NG23 lists effects on mood among the core symptoms associated with menopause, noting that they can range from minor to severe and last for short or long periods [@nice-ng23-recommendations].

Irritability rarely arrives alone. A 2026 systematic review of 12 studies in roughly 9,900 peri- and postmenopausal women found that heavier [hot flash and night sweat](/symptoms/hot-flashes/) burden was linked with worse mood symptoms, and several of the included studies suggested the connection runs partly through disrupted sleep rather than hormones alone [@neda-stepan-vms-mood-systematic-review-2026]. In practice, that means a bad night of [night sweats](/symptoms/night-sweats/) or [insomnia](/symptoms/insomnia/) can make the next day's irritability noticeably worse.

## What else can contribute

Irritability in midlife is rarely explained by hormones alone. Before assuming it's "just perimenopause," it's worth considering:

- **Chronic sleep loss** from night sweats or insomnia, which lowers tolerance for frustration on its own
- **Underlying anxiety or depression**, which can present as irritability rather than sadness or worry
- **Thyroid changes**, which can affect mood and energy
- **Caregiving load, work stress, or other life pressures** that coincide with, but aren't caused by, perimenopause

## What can help (graded by evidence)

| Option | Evidence grade | What guidelines say |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Cognitive behavioral therapy (CBT)** | :grade{id="cbt-mood"} | Level I evidence from The Menopause Society (2023); NICE also recommends menopause-specific CBT for mood symptoms linked to vasomotor symptoms [@nams-2023-nonhormone-therapy; @nice-ng23-recommendations] |
| **Treating night sweats and sleep loss** | :grade{id="treat-vms-for-mood"} | A 2026 systematic review found mood symptoms were linked with vasomotor burden, partly through sleep disruption; see [hormone therapy](/treatments/hormone-therapy/) and [non-hormonal options](/treatments/nonhormonal/) [@neda-stepan-vms-mood-systematic-review-2026] |
| **Over-the-counter herbal "calming" or "hormone balance" supplements** | :grade{id="otc-mood-supplements"} | Not recommended by The Menopause Society (2023), Levels I–II [@nams-2023-nonhormone-therapy] |
