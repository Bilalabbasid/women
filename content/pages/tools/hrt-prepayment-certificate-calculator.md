An HRT PPC (HRT prescription prepayment certificate) costs **£19.80** and covers listed hormone replacement therapy (HRT) medicines for 12 months in England [@nhsbsa-hrt-ppc; @govuk-get-a-ppc-hrt]. Each item otherwise costs £9.90, so it pays off once you'd buy 3 or more HRT items in a year [@govuk-get-a-ppc-hrt]. Prescriptions are free in Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland [@nhsinform-prescription-charges; @gov-wales-free-prescriptions; @nidirect-help-health-costs].

## How the HRT PPC calculator works

The calculator adds up a year of prescription costs three ways and shows the cheapest, using the 2026/27 prices for England (checked November 15, 2026) [@govuk-get-a-ppc; @govuk-get-a-ppc-hrt; @govuk-prescription-charges-frozen-2026]:

- **Paying for each item**: every HRT and other item × £9.90 [@govuk-get-a-ppc].
- **HRT PPC**: £19.80, plus £9.90 for each item that isn't on the HRT list [@nhsbsa-hrt-ppc].
- **12-month PPC**: £114.50, covering all your NHS prescriptions, HRT included [@nhsbsa-ppc].

It also shows four 3-month PPCs (£32.05 each) for comparison, though over a full year they cost more than one 12-month PPC [@nhsbsa-ppc].

**Count each medicine each time you collect it**, because the charge is per item, not per prescription [@nhs-ppc-save-money]. **Example.** You collect estradiol gel and Utrogestan every 3 months: that's 8 HRT items a year. Paying per item costs £79.20. The HRT PPC costs £19.80, a saving of £59.40.

Nothing you enter is saved or sent anywhere.

## HRT PPC prices in England, 2026/27

The government froze prescription charges and PPC prices for 2026/27 [@govuk-prescription-charges-frozen-2026].

| Option | Price | What it covers |
|---|---|---|
| Single prescription item | £9.90 [@govuk-get-a-ppc] | One item [@nhs-ppc-save-money] |
| HRT PPC, 12 months | £19.80 [@nhsbsa-hrt-ppc] | Unlimited listed HRT medicines [@nhsbsa-hrt-ppc] |
| PPC, 3 months | £32.05 [@nhsbsa-ppc] | All NHS prescriptions [@nhsbsa-ppc] |
| PPC, 12 months | £114.50 [@nhsbsa-ppc] | All NHS prescriptions, payable in 10 monthly Direct Debits [@nhsbsa-ppc] |

## Is the HRT PPC worth it?

Yes, if you'd pay for more than 2 HRT items in 12 months [@govuk-get-a-ppc-hrt]. At exactly 2 items it costs the same as paying per item, because £19.80 is the price of two single items [@nhsbsa-hrt-ppc].

If you also pay for other medicines, a standard PPC may save more. GOV.UK says to consider one if you need 12 or more items in 12 months and at least 10 of them aren't eligible HRT, or 4 or more items in 3 months with at least 2 not eligible HRT [@govuk-get-a-ppc-hrt]. A 12-month PPC covers HRT too, so you wouldn't need both [@nhsbsa-ppc].

## What does the HRT PPC cover?

It covers an unlimited number of the HRT medicines on the NHSBSA list for 12 months, whatever they're prescribed for [@nhsbsa-hrt-ppc]. It doesn't cover other prescriptions, and it doesn't cover every HRT medicine: only those also licensed in the UK to treat menopause [@nhsbsa-hrt-ppc; @nhsbsa-hrt-ppc-medicines].

- **On the list**: many estradiol (oestrogen) gels, patches and tablets, such as Oestrogel, Evorel, Sandrena and Elleste; micronized progesterone (Utrogestan); vaginal estrogen, such as Vagifem and estriol cream; and tibolone [@nhsbsa-hrt-ppc-medicines].
- **Not on the list**: testosterone [@nhsbsa-hrt-ppc-medicines].
- **No charge anyway**: the Mirena coil, because it's a contraceptive [@nhsbsa-hrt-ppc-medicines].
- **Generic versions**: covered when the generic name is on the prescription [@nhsbsa-hrt-ppc-medicines].

The list changes from time to time, so [check your medicine on the NHSBSA list](https://www.nhsbsa.nhs.uk/help-nhs-prescription-costs/nhs-hormone-replacement-therapy-prescription-prepayment-certificate-hrt-ppc/medicines-covered-hrt-ppc) or ask your pharmacist [@nhsbsa-hrt-ppc-medicines]. For what each type of HRT is, see [types of HRT](/treatments/hormone-therapy/types/).

## How to buy an HRT PPC

- **Online**: through the NHSBSA "Buy an HRT PPC" service; the certificate can be emailed, printed or downloaded [@nhsbsa-hrt-ppc].
- **At a pharmacy**: some pharmacies sell the HRT PPC [@nhsbsa-hrt-ppc; @nhs-ppc-save-money].
- **Help**: the NHSBSA line for HRT PPC questions is 0300 330 2089 [@nhsbsa-hrt-ppc].

## When does an HRT PPC start, and can you backdate it?

You choose the start date, which can be up to one calendar month before or after the day you buy it [@nhsbsa-hrt-ppc]. It's valid for 12 months from that date, including the day it expires [@nhsbsa-hrt-ppc]. If your next HRT prescription is more than a month away, NHSBSA suggests buying nearer the time [@nhsbsa-hrt-ppc].

## Refunds and claiming back charges

- **The HRT PPC itself**: not refundable, even if your circumstances change [@nhsbsa-hrt-ppc].
- **Charges paid while you were covered**: ask the pharmacy for an FP57 refund form when you pay, because you can't get one later, and apply within 3 months [@nhsbsa-hrt-ppc].
- **A standard PPC**: you may get a refund if you become entitled to free prescriptions while it's valid [@nhsbsa-ppc].

## Who gets free prescriptions in England?

You don't need any certificate if you already get free prescriptions [@nhsbsa-free-prescriptions]. In England, that includes people who are:

- **By age**: 60 or over, under 16, or 16 to 18 and in full-time education [@nhsbsa-free-prescriptions].
- **With a certificate**: a valid maternity exemption certificate (pregnant or had a baby in the last 12 months), medical exemption certificate, HC2 certificate or war pension exemption certificate [@nhsbsa-free-prescriptions].
- **On some benefits**: Universal Credit with take-home pay under the limit, or Pension Credit Guarantee Credit [@nhsbsa-free-prescriptions].

## Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland

You can only get a PPC if you live in England [@govuk-get-a-ppc]. Elsewhere in the UK you don't need one:

- **Scotland**: prescriptions are free [@nhsinform-prescription-charges].
- **Wales**: free if you're registered with a Welsh GP; if you live in Wales with a GP in England, you get an entitlement card to show at the pharmacy [@gov-wales-free-prescriptions].
- **Northern Ireland**: all prescriptions dispensed there are free for everyone [@nidirect-help-health-costs].
