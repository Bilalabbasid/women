Appointments are short. Selecting your priorities ahead of time helps you cover what matters most — whether you want to ask how the benefit-risk profile of hormone therapy applies under age 60 or within 10 years of menopause [@nams-2022-hormone-therapy], what the 2025–2026 FDA boxed-warning changes mean [@fda-mht-labeling-request-2025], or what monitoring non-hormonal options like fezolinetant require [@veozah-prescribing-information].

::tool{id="treatment-questions"}

## How to pair this sheet with your symptom log

1. **Bring two numbers:** the date of your last period (or how much your cycle length has shifted) and how often your most disruptive symptom happens per day or week. You can log both in our [private symptom tracker](/tools/symptom-tracker/).
2. **State your goal in the first minute:** for example, *"Night sweats are waking me three times a night and I want to compare transdermal hormone therapy with non-hormonal options."*
3. **Read the background before you go:** see our guides on [what the FDA's HRT boxed-warning change means](/treatments/hormone-therapy/boxed-warning/), [evidence-graded options for hot flashes](/symptoms/hot-flashes/), and [questions to ask your doctor about perimenopause](/perimenopause/questions-for-your-doctor/).

## When to get help now

:::urgent
- **Any bleeding after 12 months without a period** should be checked by a doctor [@nhs-postmenopausal-bleeding].
- **Palpitations that do not go away, or come with chest pain, breathlessness, or fainting:** call 911 (US) or 999 (UK) [@nhs-heart-palpitations].
- **If you are thinking about harming yourself:** call or text **988** (US) [@lifeline-988] or call **116 123** (Samaritans UK) [@samaritans-contact], free and confidential 24/7.
:::
