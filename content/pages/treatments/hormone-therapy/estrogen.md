**Estrogen** is the hormone whose decline during [perimenopause](/perimenopause/) and [menopause](/menopause/) drives most of the classic symptoms — [hot flashes](/symptoms/hot-flashes/), [night sweats](/symptoms/night-sweats/), and [vaginal dryness](/symptoms/vaginal-dryness/) among them. Replacing it is the core of [hormone therapy (HRT)](/treatments/hormone-therapy/), and it's graded :grade{id="mht-vms"} on our scale. The Menopause Society calls it the most effective treatment for vasomotor symptoms and genitourinary syndrome of menopause, and it also prevents bone loss [@nams-2022-hormone-therapy].

## Estrogen alone, or estrogen with a progestogen?

This is determined by one simple factor: whether you still have a uterus [@nice-ng23-recommendations].

- **Estrogen-only HRT** is offered to people who've had a **total hysterectomy**. With no uterine lining left, there's nothing for unopposed estrogen to overstimulate.
- **Combined HRT** (estrogen plus a progestogen) is offered to people who still have a uterus, because estrogen alone can thicken the uterine lining over time, raising endometrial cancer risk — the progestogen protects against that. See our [progesterone and progestogens](/treatments/hormone-therapy/progesterone/) page for more.

This isn't a preference call — it's a safety requirement based on your anatomy.

## Forms and dose

Estrogen is available as pills, patches, gels, sprays, and (in low-dose vaginal form) creams, tablets, and rings for local symptoms only — see our dedicated [vaginal estrogen](/treatments/hormone-therapy/vaginal-estrogen/) page for that distinct use case. For systemic use, NICE recommends using the **lowest effective dose** [@nice-ng23-recommendations], and the route you choose changes more than convenience — see [HRT patch vs. pill](/treatments/hormone-therapy/patch-vs-pill/) for how it affects blood clot and stroke risk specifically.

## Where this fits in the bigger picture

Estrogen's benefits and risks — breast cancer, blood clots, stroke — vary by type, route, and your personal history.
