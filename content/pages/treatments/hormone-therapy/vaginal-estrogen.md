**Low-dose vaginal estrogen** (available as vaginal creams, inserts/tablets, and flexible rings) is graded :grade{id="vaginal-estrogen-gsm"} on our scale for treating moderate-to-severe genitourinary syndrome of menopause (GSM) — including [vaginal dryness](/symptoms/vaginal-dryness/), [painful sex](/symptoms/painful-sex/), and [recurrent urinary tract infections](/symptoms/recurrent-utis/) — when non-hormonal moisturisers are not sufficient [@nams-2020-gsm; @cochrane-vaginal-moisturizers].

## How it compares with non-hormonal moisturisers and lubricants

Clinical guidelines from The Menopause Society (2020) and ACOG (2021) recommend a stepped or combined approach [@nams-2020-gsm; @acog-clinical-consensus-gsm]:

1. **First-line non-hormonal care:** Regular [vaginal moisturisers and personal lubricants](/care/moisturizers-and-lubricants/) hydrate vaginal tissue and reduce friction during sex [@acog-clinical-consensus-gsm; @cochrane-vaginal-moisturizers].
2. **Low-dose local vaginal estrogen:** When moderate-to-severe dryness, burning, or dyspareunia persists, low-dose vaginal estrogen restores vaginal mucosal maturation, tissue elasticity, and natural acidic pH [@nams-2020-gsm; @cochrane-vaginal-moisturizers].

Unlike [systemic hormone therapy](/treatments/hormone-therapy/), low-dose vaginal estrogen acts locally in the urogenital tissues rather than raising systemic blood estradiol levels above normal postmenopausal ranges [@nams-2020-gsm].

## The 2025–2026 FDA boxed-warning removal

Historically, low-dose vaginal estrogen carried the same FDA boxed warning for cardiovascular disease, breast cancer, and dementia as systemic oral hormone therapy — a warning that deterred many women from filling prescriptions. In November 2025 and February 2026, the FDA requested and approved removing those boxed warnings from vaginal estrogen labels [@fda-mht-labeling-changes-2026], a move explicitly endorsed by both **The Menopause Society** [@menopause-society-fda-comment-2025] and the **International Menopause Society** [@ims-statement-boxed-warning-2025]. Read the full regulatory story in [The FDA HRT boxed warning change, explained](/treatments/hormone-therapy/boxed-warning/).
