**Veozah (fezolinetant)** is a once-a-day, non-hormonal pill for moderate to severe hot flashes and night sweats caused by menopause [@veozah-prescribing-information]. It can harm the liver, so the label requires blood tests before starting and through month 9 [@veozah-prescribing-information]. Hormone therapy works best, but Veozah has top-level evidence among non-hormonal options [@nams-2023-nonhormone-therapy].

## What is Veozah used for?

Veozah is used to treat moderate to severe hot flashes and night sweats (hot flushes in the UK) caused by menopause, which doctors call vasomotor symptoms [@veozah-prescribing-information]. That is the only use on its FDA label. It was first approved in the US in 2023 and is distributed by Astellas [@veozah-prescribing-information].

In the UK it is sold as Veoza. Since March 2026, NICE says it can be used on the NHS in England when hormone replacement therapy (HRT) is unsuitable [@nice-ta1143-fezolinetant-2026].

## How does Veozah work?

As estrogen falls during menopause, a group of brain cells that help set body temperature becomes overactive. These cells release too much of a chemical messenger called neurokinin B, which upsets the brain's temperature control and sets off [hot flashes](/symptoms/hot-flashes/) and [night sweats](/symptoms/night-sweats/) [@nams-2023-nonhormone-therapy]. Veozah blocks the spot where neurokinin B attaches to these cells (the NK3 receptor) [@veozah-prescribing-information].

Veozah is not a hormone, and in studies it did not clearly change estrogen or other sex hormone levels [@veozah-prescribing-information]. It is also not an antidepressant: it belongs to its own class of NK3 receptor blockers [@veozah-prescribing-information].

## Does Veozah work? What the trials showed

Yes. In the trials, it cut the number of moderate to severe hot flashes more than a placebo did. The FDA approval rested on two 12-week randomized trials in 1,022 women who had at least 7 moderate to severe hot flashes a day [@veozah-prescribing-information]:

- **Fewer hot flashes**: Women started with about 10 to 12 a day. At week 12, the 45 mg dose cut that by about 2.5 more hot flashes a day than placebo, a difference the label calls clinically meaningful [@veozah-prescribing-information].
- **Milder hot flashes**: Severity also dropped more than with placebo at weeks 4 and 12 [@veozah-prescribing-information].
- **Longer-term safety**: Safety was studied in three 52-week trials in which 1,100 women took Veozah [@veozah-prescribing-information].

The Menopause Society's 2023 nonhormone position statement recommends fezolinetant for hot flashes at Level I, its top level of evidence [@nams-2023-nonhormone-therapy]. On our scale, fezolinetant for hot flashes and night sweats is graded :grade{id="fezolinetant-vms"}.

## How long does it take for Veozah to work?

It can start to help within the first week. In one of the two main trials, hot flashes were already less frequent and less severe than with placebo after 1 week, and the benefit lasted through 52 weeks [@lederman-skylight1-2023]. The label reports clear improvement by week 4, the first time point it measured, and at week 12 [@veozah-prescribing-information].

## Veozah side effects

The most common Veozah side effects are stomach pain, diarrhea, trouble sleeping, back pain and hot flashes, each reported by at least 2% of women and more often than with placebo [@veozah-prescribing-information]. Raised liver enzymes on blood tests were also more common [@veozah-prescribing-information]. These are the rates from the 52-week placebo-controlled trial:

| Side effect (52-week trial) | Veozah 45 mg | Placebo |
|---|---|---|
| Stomach (abdominal) pain | 4.3% [@veozah-prescribing-information] | 2.1% [@veozah-prescribing-information] |
| Diarrhea | 3.9% [@veozah-prescribing-information] | 2.6% [@veozah-prescribing-information] |
| Trouble sleeping (insomnia) | 3.9% [@veozah-prescribing-information] | 1.8% [@veozah-prescribing-information] |
| Back pain | 3.0% [@veozah-prescribing-information] | 2.1% [@veozah-prescribing-information] |
| Hot flashes | 2.5% [@veozah-prescribing-information] | 1.6% [@veozah-prescribing-information] |
| Raised liver enzymes | 2.3% [@veozah-prescribing-information] | 0.8% [@veozah-prescribing-information] |

The serious side effect to know about is liver injury, covered in the next section [@veozah-prescribing-information].

### Does Veozah help with sleep, or make you sleepy?

Sleepiness is not on the label's side effect list, but trouble sleeping is: 3.9% of women on Veozah against 1.8% on placebo [@veozah-prescribing-information]. On the other hand, early research suggests drugs of this type may help night waking and sleep quality [@nams-2023-nonhormone-therapy]. The label makes no claim about sleep.

### Does Veozah help with mood swings?

There isn't good evidence either way yet. The Menopause Society says the effects of these drugs on mood related to hot flashes haven't been studied enough [@nams-2023-nonhormone-therapy].

## Veozah liver warning: which blood tests, and when

In December 2024 the FDA added a boxed warning to Veozah's label because liver injury has been reported in people taking it since it came on the market [@veozah-prescribing-information]. In those reports, liver injury appeared within 40 days of starting, and symptoms gradually got better after people stopped Veozah [@veozah-prescribing-information].

The label sets out these steps [@veozah-prescribing-information]:

- **Before starting**: Liver blood tests (ALT, AST, alkaline phosphatase and bilirubin). Veozah isn't started if liver enzymes or bilirubin are already twice the top of the normal range or higher.
- **Follow-up tests**: Monthly for the first 3 months, then at 6 months and 9 months, and any time symptoms suggest liver injury.
- **When to stop**: If liver enzymes rise above 5 times the top of the normal range, or above 3 times while bilirubin is above 2 times. If enzymes rise above 3 times normal, testing is done more often until they settle.

In the trials, liver enzymes rose above 3 times the top of the normal range in 2.3% of women taking Veozah and 0.9% taking placebo. Most had no symptoms, and levels returned to or near normal [@veozah-prescribing-information]. That's why the label wants every scheduled test done, even if you feel well.

## Who should not take Veozah?

According to the label, Veozah must not be used by people with [@veozah-prescribing-information]:

- **Known cirrhosis**: Scarring of the liver.
- **Severe kidney problems**: Severe kidney disease or kidney failure.
- **Certain other medicines**: Medicines that block a liver enzyme called CYP1A2 raise the amount of Veozah in the blood. The label's studies included fluvoxamine, mexiletine and cimetidine. Tell your prescriber and pharmacist about every prescription, over-the-counter medicine and supplement you take.

Safety and effectiveness haven't been established in people under 18, and the trials didn't include enough women over 65 to know whether they respond differently [@veozah-prescribing-information]. There are no data on Veozah in pregnancy or breastfeeding [@veozah-prescribing-information].

### Can you take Veozah with estrogen?

The label doesn't address combining Veozah with hormone therapy, and the main trials didn't test it: women who had been using hormone therapy stopped it before joining [@veozah-prescribing-information]. Ask your prescriber before using both.

## How to take Veozah

The labeled dose is one 45 mg tablet by mouth once a day, with or without food, at about the same time each day. Tablets are swallowed whole with liquid, not cut, crushed or chewed [@veozah-prescribing-information]. Your prescriber decides whether Veozah suits you.

- **Missed dose**: Take it as soon as possible that day, unless the next dose is less than 12 hours away. Go back to your usual time the next day [@veozah-prescribing-information].
- **Stopping**: The label gives no tapering schedule, and The Menopause Society lists Veozah as a single daily dose with no step-up needed [@veozah-prescribing-information; @nams-2023-nonhormone-therapy]. Talk to your prescriber before stopping, and stop right away if you have signs of liver injury (see below).

## How much does Veozah cost? Insurance, Medicare and savings

Without insurance, Veozah's retail price was $700.44 for 30 tablets (a month at the labeled dose), with GoodRx coupon prices from $484.75, as of November 14, 2026 [@goodrx-veozah-prices-2026-11-14]. There is no generic version [@goodrx-veozah-prices-2026-11-14]. Prices change often, so check before you fill a prescription.

- **Insurance and Medicare**: GoodRx says some Medicare and insurance plans cover Veozah [@goodrx-veozah-prices-2026-11-14]. Each Medicare drug plan has its own list of covered drugs (formulary) and cost tiers, and your prescriber can ask for an exception if Veozah isn't covered [@medicare-how-drug-plans-work].
- **Manufacturer savings program**: Astellas runs a copay program for people with commercial insurance, capped at $4,000 a year. It can't be used with Medicare, Medicaid, TRICARE, VA or other government coverage, or by people paying cash [@veozah-savings-program-terms-2026].
- **In the UK**: NHS England must fund it for people for whom HRT is unsuitable, when it's the most suitable option [@nice-ta1143-fezolinetant-2026].

Our [guide to insurance and menopause care](/care/insurance/) covers what to ask your plan.

## Veozah vs. Lynkuet vs. hormone therapy

No trial has compared Veozah directly with other non-hormonal treatments [@nice-ta1143-fezolinetant-2026]. Hormone therapy remains the most effective treatment for hot flashes, and non-hormonal options matter most for people who can't take hormones or prefer not to [@nams-2023-nonhormone-therapy]. Here is how the two neurokinin blockers compare on their labels:

| Feature | Veozah (fezolinetant) | Lynkuet (elinzanetant) |
|---|---|---|
| What it blocks | Neurokinin B only (NK3) [@veozah-prescribing-information] | Neurokinin B and substance P (NK3 and NK1) [@lynkuet-prescribing-information] |
| Labeled dose | One 45 mg tablet once daily [@veozah-prescribing-information] | Two 60 mg capsules once daily at bedtime [@lynkuet-prescribing-information] |
| Boxed warning | Yes, for liver injury [@veozah-prescribing-information] | None [@lynkuet-prescribing-information] |
| Liver tests | Before starting, monthly for 3 months, then at 6 and 9 months [@veozah-prescribing-information] | Before starting and at 3 months [@lynkuet-prescribing-information] |
| Most common side effects | Stomach pain, diarrhea, trouble sleeping, back pain [@veozah-prescribing-information] | Headache, tiredness, dizziness, sleepiness [@lynkuet-prescribing-information] |
| Pregnancy | No human data [@veozah-prescribing-information] | Must not be used [@lynkuet-prescribing-information] |
| Retail price, 30-day supply (Nov 14, 2026) | $700.44 [@goodrx-veozah-prices-2026-11-14] | $781.98 [@goodrx-lynkuet-prices-2026-11-14] |

Side effect rates from different drugs' trials can't be compared directly [@veozah-prescribing-information]. Read more in our [Lynkuet (elinzanetant) guide](/treatments/nonhormonal/elinzanetant/), compare all [non-hormonal treatments for hot flashes](/treatments/nonhormonal/), or see whether [hormone therapy could suit you](/treatments/hormone-therapy/is-it-right-for-me/). With hormone therapy, people who still have a uterus need a progestogen along with estrogen [@owh-menopause-treatment-2026].

## When to see a doctor

:::urgent
**Stop taking Veozah and get medical care right away, including liver blood tests, if you notice any possible sign of liver injury** [@veozah-prescribing-information]:

- New tiredness or loss of appetite
- Nausea or vomiting
- Itching
- Yellowing of the skin or the whites of the eyes (jaundice)
- Pale stools or dark urine
- Stomach (abdominal) pain
:::

Also talk to your prescriber before starting or stopping any other medicine, because some can interact with Veozah [@veozah-prescribing-information]. Keep every scheduled liver test, even if you feel well [@veozah-prescribing-information].
