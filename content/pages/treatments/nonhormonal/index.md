Whether you have a medical contraindication to estrogen (such as a history of [breast cancer](/groups/breast-cancer-survivors/) or blood clots) or simply prefer a non-hormonal approach, you do not have to rely on unproven supplements. The Menopause Society's **2023 Nonhormone Therapy Position Statement** grades the clinical trial evidence across prescription medications, behavioral therapies, and over-the-counter remedies for [hot flashes](/symptoms/hot-flashes/) and [night sweats](/symptoms/night-sweats/) [@nams-2023-nonhormone-therapy].
