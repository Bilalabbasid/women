# Three visual directions

Mockups: `docs/ui-review/directions/*.html` (open in a browser; dark mode follows the OS setting).
Screenshots: `docs/ui-review/directions/shots/` at 375 px (hero first screen + article top) and 1280 px (full),
light and dark. Each mock shows the homepage hero (question → chip picker → quiz CTA) and the top of the
night-sweats article (short answer, Try tonight, evidence rows with grade meters, sticky TOC, text-size control).

All three share the same structure, the 5-step grade meter (icon + label, never colour alone), a low-glare dark theme
(no pure black or pure white), and 48 px tap targets. They differ in type, palette, spacing and one signature detail.

| | A · Reading Room | B · Evening Edition | C · Field Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Feel | A good medical library: quiet, ordered, grown-up | A thoughtful weekend magazine: warmer, more voice | A modern clinician's notebook: crisp, direct |
| Headings | Newsreader (optical-size serif) | Fraunces, "soft" axis (rounded serif) | Bricolage Grotesque (warm grotesque) |
| Body | Atkinson Hyperlegible Next (designed for low vision) | Source Sans 3 | Literata (screen-reading serif) |
| Anchor | **Brand green #1F5A4C**, brass for "Try tonight" | Ink indigo #27365A + amber | Moss #3C5E4C + clay |
| Dark theme | Deep green-black #141B19, warm parchment text | Dusk blue #181B23, amber accents | Warm charcoal #1A1815, sage accent |
| Spacing | Generous, square-ish (6 px radii), ruled | Roomy, round (pills, 16 px radii) | Tight-ish, notebook-square (4 px radii) |
| Signature | Journal masthead double rule + **§-numbered** sections and TOC | **Pull-quote Short answer** in the serif, big italic step numerals, dusk horizon on desktop | **Transition thread**: a timeline line with stops down the article margin; grade meter as stepping stones |
| Risk | Closest to today's site, so the change is less dramatic | Indigo + amber moves away from the brand green; amber CTA in dark | Serif body text is less legible than a hyperlegible sans for some low-vision readers |

**Recommendation: A, borrowing two things from B.** A keeps #1F5A4C, and Atkinson Hyperlegible is the strongest
body face for changing eyesight. From B, take the large serif "Short answer" pull-quote and the italic step numerals
for "Try tonight"; they add warmth without leaving the library feel.
