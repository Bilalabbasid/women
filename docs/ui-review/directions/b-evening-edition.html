Night sweats: how to sleep cooler tonight

Not medical advice. Written from published sources with AI help, not by a doctor. How this page was made

Checked against 4 sources · Last checked 2 Nov 2026 · 6 min read

Short answer

Night sweats are hot flashes that happen while you sleep. They’re one of the most common signs of perimenopause, and you’re not imagining them. A cooler room, layered bedding and avoiding alcohol in the evening help tonight. Hormone therapy has the strongest evidence if they keep waking you.

Try tonight

  1. Set the bedroom to about 18 °C (65 °F).
  2. Swap the duvet for two light layers you can kick off.
  3. Skip wine after dinner; it’s a common trigger.

What has the best evidence

Hormone therapy (HRT)
●Strong
